WOW! What a day! A couple of big things happened today. I'll write everything in order.

First of all today, we did a little bit of door to door on Robert-Koch Str. Nothing too exciting happened there.

Then we came home for lunch and I got a letter from Dad, B, G&G Shepherd. I made ravioli and everything else went well. Dad chewed me out for not writing. I think it was just when I sent the package, it skipped a week of them getting a letter. Nothing big!

After that we did a little S.K. (street contacting) until our appointment with Herr R. After talking and getting to know him better, and making an appointment, we walked slowly to Schwester B's.

Institute went well. I participated more than usual. That was cool. I also finished off two bowls of snacks like usual.

After that, we went to D's. And there we played with M and T and I BOUGHT A BIKE! We also got some food. I was happy to get the bike. It's cool.

Then for the big surprise for today! Elders H and A both got transferred! That's a real big bummer! We don't know yet if two new Elders are coming or if Elder C and I will be the only ones here in Halberstadt. It's sad, but that's the life of a missionary. I was really glad I didn't get transferred.
