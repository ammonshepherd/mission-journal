Today went pretty o.k., good. We sang this morning with Brd. J, because we missed it on Sunday. After that I made cookies for people. Now we have to figure out who exactly those people are. Two we know for sure are ourselves.

After the cookies where done, we went to Sch. F. We could only stay for 1/2 hour because we had to catch the train to QLB. We caught the train and got there no problem. We tried one contact and then visited E N for a while. After that we came back home and fixed a lot of food, because we had had no Mittags. After that I read a little bit in the Ensign and did the dishes, and then filled out more on the new contact list. That was the whole day.

It felt like Sunday today. Mainly because we sang with Brd. J, and because the town was empty. The reason for that is because today was a holiday. Germany was reunited today 7 years ago or so.

    It was October 3, 1990, so only 6 years earlier.
