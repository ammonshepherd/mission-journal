I'm writing this on Thursday so I'll make it short and sweet. On Tuesday we went to Quedlinburg. We did Tür zu Tür on Wall Straße and made a few appointments. Two with girls age 15-18. And one with an older lady. We also left a Book of Mormon for Herr R. Hopefully, he will read it and want to really become active. Then we came home, ate, and did Straße Kontakt (street contacting) and checked up on old contacts until Institute. Institute went fine. Afterwards we went to Herr F's house. He was the guy in the garage that was carrying 10 beers. He had company, so we'll visit him another time.
