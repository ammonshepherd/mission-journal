Today was a day that will go gown in the History books as the day to end all days! What do I mean by that? you ask yourself. Well, I mean absolutely nothing! Today was an absolutely normal summer Sunday. Church and everything went fine. We ate at P's today. As always, there was plenty to eat, so I went away stuffed. After eating at P's we came home and prepared our talks for Zone Conference. We did that for three hours and then met with Frau R. We simply got a time frame to come back in, in which we can hopefully reach her, and maybe teach her. That was really the only kind of seek and find missionary work that we did today. Then we came home and sang with Bruder J. That was all that happened today.
