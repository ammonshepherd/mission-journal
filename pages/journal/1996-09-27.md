---
title: "I screwed up big time"
date: 1996-09-27
location: Halberstadt
template: post
---

It was just one of those days today. First of all, we were a little bit rushed this morning getting to the train. I figured everything was going normal. I later found out otherwise. Everything with the train ride went normal until after we left Wegeleben. After a while I kind of noticed that all of the scenery was different, and that we were going through different towns. At one of them, I got out and looked at the destination signs on the train and found out that we were on a train to Halle instead of Quedlinburg. We then decided to get out at Aschersleben and take the train from Aschersleben to QLB. We accomplished that without any problems. The only thing was that the train to QLB wasn't really a train. It was just a one car thing, like a bus on the tracks. It also took an hour and a half to get to QLB.

When we finally did get to QLB we had an hour before our appointment with N, so we went by on a few of our contacts. We actually got a hold of three people. Two said not to come back, but we one did. After that we went to our appointment with N. When we got there, nobody was home, so we went and bought something at the Bäckerei. After that we went back, but still nobody was home. As we were writing a note to leave, N drove up. He had had to work late, but we were able to talk for a good hour or so. We had a good talk with him, and hopefully we can help him out. Because we stayed so long by N, we couldn't keep our Termin with E.N. We called him and made out a new appointment. Then we came home. Just in time for Talente Abend. That went OK. It was held in the chapel, because there is no other place. We did a lot that normally wouldn't be done in an American chapel. Lots of clapping, laughing, stuff like that. It all went well until it was my turn. I really screwed up big time. I had to stand up and play, so that didn't help any. I was also really nervous. That was the first time I sand and played at the same time, and sang all the way through. I didn't sing loud enough, and a billion other things I did wrong. Oh, well. So is life. It was a lot of fun, though. Talente Abend, I mean. I played a lot with the kids again.

That's everything, I think, from today.
