Day 100! Wow. A long time. Many things learned. Cool. 100th Day.

Even though today was a landmark day, it went by pretty normal. It still was different, though. We tried to get copies of the Tür zu Tür Berichts (reports) this morning. We went all over town and finally ended up at Kaufland. We got about 20 copies for 5DM. We should have gotten 15 for 2DM, but it got a little messed up, the machine that is. He also looked at bikes this morning. We figured if I bought a bike when Elder C got his back, that we could ride to Quedlinburg and to other places close by, and we could save some money. Also we'd be able to do bike tours and a whole bunch of stuff. There's a nice one at Kaufland for 220DM, but it looks kind of cheap. We saw a Giant for 499DM. It's loaded and is a very, very nice bike. It would last me for a few years. I'll keep looking around, but right now my choices are between these two.

After Mittags we spent a few hours copying the TzT Berichts from the Green books to the regular TzT Berichts. We used all 20 copies and aren't even halfway done.

After that we did a little more SK (street contacting) and Menschen besuchen (looking for contacts). We met up with Br. J and he bought us an ice. We also visited Br and Sis P. They were very happy to have us visit them for no reason. We'll have to do that more often with the members. Well, that's all for today.
