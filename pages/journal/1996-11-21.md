Today went pretty well. We had Districts Versammlung in Rahlstedt today. Elder B tried to take a picture of the pretty girl we always see. He acted like he was taking a picture of me, and when she walked by he took the picture. Unfortunately, the camera didn't work and the picture didn't take. Anyhow, DV was pretty fun. My Deutsch Theme sucked because I didn't really have time to prepare anything.

After that we had lunch at Sch. F's. It was a really good meal. After that we visited R for an half hour or so. She's doing better, except that she smoked once today and said that she would smoke another one tonight so that she could take a break at work. I hope she'll start taking it a little more seriously so that there won't be any problems. It seems that the only people that are investigators are weird, not normal people.

Anyhow, after that we went to Sport Abend. Again there wasn't too many people, but this time we played soccer anyhow. That was a lot of fun. We also played basketball. Elder B and I played two on two with C and A. At first they were winning pretty good, but then after they hit 18 or so and we had 9, we then turned up the heat and totally wasted them. We stopped at 40 for us and in the 20's for them. It was cool! We only won, I think, because we played harder than they did. After Sport Abend we came home and made some calls. I am pretty sore.

Oh yea, on the way home from D.V. I bought an Asterix & Obelix.
