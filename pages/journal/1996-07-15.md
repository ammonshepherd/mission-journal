date: 1996-07-15

Well, this day was nothing like I expected. The morning all went like normal until about an hour before we were going to leave for Göttingen. We then got a phone call from President and he said that Elder C was being transferred to Göttingen and that Elder C was coming here. One of the members had called President and said that there was or there might become a problem with Elder C and B. They said they knew nothing yet has happened and probably never will, but just as a precaution, they thought Elder C needed to be transferred. So he had an hour to pack most of his things and then we had to take the train to Göttingen. So it was all a big hurry and a big surprise.

We all didn't do too much in Göttingen. We had pancakes and spoke about the problem of not everyone knowing the discussions.

Then Elder C and I came back to Halberstadt. He has a big suitcase that was a pain to carry home. And we had to walk home from the Bahnhof. By the time we finally did get home an hour later, I was sweating profusely. We still sang with Br. J. That was pretty cool. And that was the whole day.

I have no idea what kind of feelings I should be having. Elder C was cool and a good friend. But he really did need to be transferred. I think that was an answer to my prayer. From the start, I knew I needed to help Elder C out, and I really tried. I think he was just too far gone. We talked a couple of times, but that didn't seem to do anything. I prayed and prayed that I would be able to have strength to talk to him just all out, and tell him what he was doing was wrong and all, but I just couldn't do it. So then I prayed that I would get help. I told Heavenly Father that I just couldn't do it. So I think this transfer is an answer to that prayer. It's not at all what I had thought would happen, but it should definitely solve the problem.

The only thing is, now I feel like a big heel because I wimped out on the Lord. It was a tuff thing, and I just didn't feel good or strong enough to do what I was asked to. I guess I forgot about 1 Nephi 3:7 "For I know the Lord gives no commandments unto the children of man save he shall prepare a way for them to accomplish the things he asks of them."

Hopefully, next time I'll remember that and how much of a jerk, weeny, idiot, stupid, fool I was to let the Lord down. And then maybe I'll be able to do whatever he asks of me. I just hope He'll still trust me and give me more of the experiences that I need. Na ja, what can I say. Live and learn.
