Today was pretty much a dud. We left from Braunschweig about 9:00 and got to Halberstadt about 10:00. We came straight home and found out at about 10:40 that the Telecom had already come and didn't fix the phone. Then we helped out Frau F. We did the normal stuff, except we didn't have to buy anything. Also she showed us pictures. After that we came home and I took a shower. I really needed one. I hadn't had one since Saturday. I also had a nice shadow beard.

After that I filled out the rest of the contacts while Elder N prepared for Institute. After we did that for a while we went by on Herr K. We talked with him for a couple of minutes and then went to B's. We stayed there for a while and the bought Dönners on the way home. Tomorrow I find out if I'll be transferred or not. I don't really want to, but I know it'll happen soon. It's a weird feeling.
