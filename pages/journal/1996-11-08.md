Today went pretty awesome! The first thing is that my bike came. Then we taught a second, and the guy, M, knows he'll be baptized, but doesn't feel ready yet. After that, we came home and had lunch. Then we rode bikes, Yipee!, to our next appointment. It was with an older couple. We were there for 3 hours. They stuffed us with tea and cookies and stuff. We didn't get to teach them the first, because they talked the whole time. We'll come back in a month or so, hopefully we'll be able to do something then. They also gave us stuff to take home. We then came home and then left again to go by on a few contacts, and we also did TzT. We made one appointment for next week. Then we went to our next appointment to teach a first, but no one was home. After that we visited a family that have been investigators for 6 years. They're really cool. Especially because they have a picture of the Arizona Temple hanging on the wall. The husband is committed to baptism on the 8th of December, but he still smokes and has almost no time to meet with us. The family will all get baptized together, I think. At least I'll try and get it so.

Today was really cool. I know I'll really like this place, once I get to know the people and start to feel at home. Hopefully that won't take too long.

I liked riding bikes again. I think we'll do that a lot. Mal sehen.
