Today was another day stuck in the Wohnung! We did a little SK am Anfang, and then out Termin with Frau F. Her place still stinks, but oh well.

After that we came home and ate. I made Knödel and Wursten. Then we had some ice we got from Frau F. Then it was more TzT junk. Will it never end? We have a Ton of Zurükkehrens now that we can check up on. Hopefully we'll get it all organized soon.

After all that fun we went to our appointment with Herr K. It was cool! We talked with him for a long time, even had Abendbrot. He seems to have real interest. I sure hope he does, but I'm still a little skeptical. So many Kontakts only last for a few visits then, WEG! I don't like getting my hopes all up and then being disappointed. I wish we could find at least one golden. I always ask myself if we're doing all we can to help the people feel the Spirit and keep them interested in the Gospel. I wish I could let them feel exactly how I feel about everything! It would be easy if I could just do that automatic. I can't even speak German so I"ve no hope of doing it. Doch! Through prayer and faith I can do anything the LORD wants me to. I'll be praying hard for Herr K!
