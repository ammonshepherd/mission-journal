Today went pretty good. We had an appointment with Herr B in Quedlinburg. We traveled by train because Edler C's bike is screwed up. The back rim is really tweaked. So we went by train. Herr B ended up not being there because he had collapsed again and was in the hospital. So we did door to door for an hour instead. We also met with A. We made an appointment for next Thursday. Hopefully everything will go well. When we got home from Quedlinburg we had spaghetti and garlic bread at 2:00pm for Mittags. Then at 1500 we had an appointment with another man, but he didn't have time then so we made another appointment with him. We came home right then and Elder C prepared for Institute. Then we went to Institute. Today was full house. There was Sch. B, Sch. M and her friend, B, A, J, Brd. B and us. We were a little crowded, but it was all right. Institute today started off with a bang! A lot of talking about things that had nothing really to do with the topic. It was really fun for me because I tried to translate for A. I didn't do too well, but what can you expect. It was also cool because Elder C and I smelt like garlic because of the garlic bread. Na ja, all in all it was a good day. Nice and hot, too, which made me very tired the whole day. I must stop now. I have to get up at 4:30 because of Zone Conference. Bis Spater. I forgot, I also watched a mosquito suck blood out of my arm. It was cool.
