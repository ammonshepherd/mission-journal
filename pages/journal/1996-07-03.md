Wednesday was P-Day. Elder C and I got our haircuts. Then we came home and I wrote letters and what not. We played B-ball again. That was fun. After B-ball I didn't do much. We visited Br. J at 18:00. We read some tings about Abendmahl (sacrament). I also got some sandals from Br. J. They were too small for him, so he gave them to me. On the way to do more Tür zu Tür on Am Sommerbad, we met a man who was real interested in the Church. He said he would come to Church this Sunday, and try to bring some friends, too. Hopefully he does. Then we finished up Am Sommerbad.
