Today was the last day that we are allowed to go without jackets. If it stays as cool or gets cooler than it was today, then I won't mind at all.

This morning we read, sang, and ate cookies with Bruder J. After that we tried to make an appointment with Frau P, but she didn't have time then, or until two weeks. I wonder if she still has interest. After that we cam home and had mashed potatoes and würstchen for Mittags. After Mittags Pause, we did some door to door on Becker Straße. We did that until 1700 when we came home and called Frau K. She wasn't home, so we didn't get to visit the family today.

Instead we tried to see if we had any luck with zurückkehrens. The most of them were not home. We did meet with Frau K, though. We just talked with her for a while. She said that we were hübsch. I'll be with my new companion by the time we can visit her again in three weeks.

The time is flying fast. I'm glad I'm on a mission.
