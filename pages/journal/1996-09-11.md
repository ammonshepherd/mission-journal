From the weekly letter home:

When we woke up today, we went and called the A.P.'s to find out who my comp. is. Then we tried to get a hold of him, but we haven't been able to, even yet.

Now I am on the train. On my way to Magdeburg. There's nothing really special about Magdeburg. Just the fact that it's out of my mission boundaries. Why, you might ask, am I going out of my mission boundaries? The answer is simple. Actually not so simple, but here it is. Elder C has to be in Hamburg on Thursday. To make it an easier trip, the both of us are going to Braunschweig today, and staying there over night. There is another Elder going home, and he is in Braunschweig. So, the train that goes to Braunschweig goes first through Magdeburg. It just so happens that Magdeburg is out of our mission boundaries. Neat huh! (Don't worry mom, we're allowed to!)

I still have't gotten a hold of my new comp, Elder N. I will have to call him in Braunschweig and then he will come to Braunschweig tomorrow, and we will both then go to Halberstadt. I've probably made it all confusing. Don't worry, it's not all that important.

That's about all from the exciting life of the German Missionary Ammon Shepherd. I can't remember if there were any questions to be answered so I'll wait till tomorrow to finish this letter. It might be a little tale too. I'll have to send it tomorrow eveing instead of morning like usual.
</pre>
