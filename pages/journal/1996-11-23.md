Today was o.k. Not too many things were done, but we were still kept busy. This morning while I prepared my talk, Elder B translated the thing for Sch. F. AFter that we scarfed down a quick lunch and rushed out to catch the bus. We went to visit Frau W. That was a very interesting visit. She was a member from 15 years old until she was 20, and then she kicked herself out. She actually wrote a letter to David O. McKay saying that she didn't want to be a member anymore. That's sad because I don't think she understands what being a member means. When you join this Church, Christ's church, you don't just become a member. That's not really it at all. What you're really doing is making covenants with God. You're saying, I'm going to follow you, your ways, your commandments, everything. It opens the door that enables us to walk the path that goes back to God. When we stop obeying the commandments, then we put a block or another door in the hallway or path and we no longer can return and live with Heavenly Father. That's a big problem with a lot of people. They look at joining the Church just as that, no big deal. But it's way more than that.

Anyhow, we had a good long talk with her, 3 hours. After that we did a little TzT, and then came home. That was pretty much it for today.

I also got mail today. A letter from everybody but C and B and H. Pretty darn good.
