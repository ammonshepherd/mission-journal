Today was a good day. Elder C made cookies and we had Distrikt Versammlung at the same time. We had Mittags right after all that. I made Aldi's spaghetti for my first time. After that we dropped a few things by Schwester B, and then went to our appointment with H. Unfortunately, he wasn't there, so we did some TzT on Leo-Tolstoi Straße. That took a while. Then we went to visit Schwester M, but she wasn't home either. We saw A on the street and we talked with her for a minute, then we were off to Post where we mailed Elder H his tie and Elders H & M an Empfählung. Then we came home and got the cookies to take to D's. We had them on their plate and gave them cookies to say thanks for the potato salad. We ended up eating a few cheese crisps. I also got the keys to the old U-lock.

Then we visited with Frau K for a while. We gave them some cookies too.

That was all the fun for today.
