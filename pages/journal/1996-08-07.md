Today was tuff! I forgot to say my morning prayer and the whole day was messed up and hectic and terrible!

It started off with having to go shopping for two hours. At Aldi's and at Kauflang. As soon as I got home I started working on the fry bread. That took a while and I only got a letter to home started. Then we all ate fry bread. Elder C and Elder H came here. Right after fry bread we went to the garden and burned Elder C's old suit, shirt, tie, socks and shoes. That was cool. Then we played basketball for a while. After a bit of that, Elder C and I took the Straßenbahn to Hauptbahnhof and picked up his bike. We had to walk home because we weren't allowed to take it on the bahn.

When we got home, we said a quick good bye to Elders H and C and then got ready for our Termin with Frau P. Who, by the way, didn't even show up, but did drive by a couple of times in a Trabi. Na ja.

Also cool this day. I got my hair trimmed by Elder C. It looks pretty good, except he cut my sideburns too short and cut a slice through one.

I didn't get to do anything I wanted to get done today. That's another reason, besides not praying, that made today suck!
