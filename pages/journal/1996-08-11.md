date: 1996-08-11

Today was an O.K. day. Ashley came to Church. Familie D were no vacation or just out of town. Because of that Elder C and I had to play the organ in church. We both did o.k., but we still could have done better. It was hard to play on the organ because it has a smaller keyboard. My talk went well, too. I was so nervous about playing the organ that I didn't have time to be nervous about speaking. I was proud of myself, because that was the first talk that I've written straight into German, without help, too.

After Church we ate at Familie B's. That was pretty cool, because we got to know them a little better. I had fun playing with their grandson who was staying with them.

After that we came back home and tried some more to organize the other Elder's TxT Berichts. That's a terrible job, and it's taking up a lot of time. I can't wait until we're done with them.

We had a Termin with Herr W. We spoke with him for a while. Like so many Kontakts, he ended up not having any more interest.

After that we sang with Brd. J. What more can I say about that? Singing is always fun, with Brd. J it's funner. We all sound good together, too.
