Well, it's finally happened. And I'm here in Lübeck now. It was a very long trip. I left the apartment with Elder N, U, and Brd P at 6:45am. We got to the Bahnhof with plenty of time. Then I was off. I'll probably never see Halberstadt again*. I liked it there.


I missed the connection in Magdeburg, because the train stopped for an 1/2 hour in Blumenberg and it wasn't supposed to. I got another connection that went through Hannover and Hamburg. I had to umsteig (transfer) in both places. Nothing happened the whole trip except that I tripped over my bags and fell on the floor in Hannover. Otherwise everything went well. By the time I got to Lübeck my arms were dead. I met Elder B and then we took the bus to my new home. I now live in a huge Hochhaus (apartment). About 14 stories high. We live on the 7th. Luckily there is an elevator.

I had about an hour to unpack and then we were off to I's. She will be baptized in a week from Sunday. We taught her the 5th. It's pretty cool here. Lots of people to teach. That went pretty well. After that we rushed home and I had another half hour to unpack and stuff. Then it was off to Sportabend. We play with the member kids. We play soccer for an hour and then basketball for an hour. It was fun. I think soccer was actually more fun than basketball, even though I suck more at soccer than b-ball. We can only play half-court in b-ball, and with 4 people per team, it kind of sucks. After that we came home. I actually planned the next day with Elder B. It'll be different now that all the rules will be followed. I just hope I can do it now. I also hope I'll be able to fit in here. I think I'll like it here.



    I visited while doing dissertation research in 2013 or so, and then drove through with Mom and Terry in 2015.
