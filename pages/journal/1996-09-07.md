Today was a weird day. This morning we walked through the Altstadt Fest a couple of times. It was a weird feeling while we were there, for me. I only tried to talk with a few people, under 6, instead of all of the people. I just didn't have a peaceful feeling there like I do usually. The Spirit was just not there, and I guess I was scared to talk with people. We came home after that. On the way home I was pondering the feelings I was having and pleading with Heavenly Father to help us with the work and to help me. Then something cool happened. A guy in a car pulled up beside us and asked if we were Americans. He was holding up traffic so he went around the corner and parked, so we we went to talk with him. He was a big American fan, and had had earlier contact with missionaries in Hannover, where he lives. He said that he was really interested in the Church, and he is in Wernigerode for today and tomorrow, so he'll come here to Church tomorrow. We also gave him a Book of Mormon. Hopefully he will come, and Familie K also.

When we got home we ate Mittags and then Elder C finished his box and did some packing for about four hours. Then we went to Sch. M for Abendbrot. After that we came back and sang with Brd. J, we practiced for tomorrow. We also sung "I know that my Redeemer lives" and "I need thee every hour." They brought the Spirit in strong, it was awesome!

The day started sour and turned out sweet. Hopefully tomorrow will go well. God answers prayers!

From the weekly letter home:

On Saturday, the next day, a few noteworthy things happened. First thing in the morning we went to the Altstadt Fest. It was like a carnival in the main street of the city. It was weird, though. The whole time we were there I felt weird, I was scared to talk with people, and normally I'm not. I was feeling pretty bummed as we were walking home from that, because I realized I let Satan get me scared. I was also doing a lot of thinking about other things. Mostly about how slowly the work in Halberstadt is going and stuff. I was also doing a lot of praying in my heart that Heavenly Father would help us out. Then my prayers were answered! Right then. A guy pulled up in a car and wanted to talk with us. He got out of traffic so we could talk to him. He ended up coming from Hannover. He had contact with missionaries about 6 months earlier, but then stopped or something. Anyhow, he had gone to church in Hannover and he really like it. He was in Halberstadt for Sat and Sun, and really wanted to come to church. We gave him the address and everything, also a Book of Mormon. Unfortunately he never showed up at church. Hopefully he just couldn't make it, but still has interest in the church. We did get his address, so we can give that to the Elders in Hannover. I have a copy too, in case I ever serve in Hannover. It was a neat experience on Sat., to have my prayer answered like that. Oh well!

Also, Sat night was pretty awesome too. We practiced hymns with a member, who can't read too well, every Sun. We practiced on Sat., because we sang a song in sacrament the next Sunday. We sang "I Know That My Redeemer Lives" and "I Need Thee Every Hour" in addition to the song we sang in sacrament. It was awesome, because the Spirit was really strong as we sang. It was just plain cool!
