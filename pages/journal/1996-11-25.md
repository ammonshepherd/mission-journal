Well, it came today. The first snow of my first winter in Germany. It started after Mittags, and is still going, I think, with only a couple hours of stop in between. Today was pretty o.k. We taught MC the 3rd at the Church. He's kind of weird, takes everything very seriously, and doesn't really have a sense of humor. BUT he'll be baptized one of these days. The discussion went pretty well.

After that we ate and played ping-pong there. That was fun. We then rode the bus to R's. That went o.k. Something is definitely wrong with her, but she's being a stubborn German and won't tell us anything. It's so stupid! I don't know why she won't let us help her, and we can't help her if she doesn't tell us anything. She's a chick! enough said.

Anyhow, after that, D and we played in the snow that had fallen as we were in with R. We visited Sch. S in the hospital after that. Then we caught the bus to the train station and the train to Bad Oldesloe. Our meeting with S went a lot better today. We actually started teaching him the second. It goes pretty slow because there are a lot of interruptions. That's the devil's fault, so he must be important. We only got through the 3rd principle today. We'll come back though. It'll take some work, but in the end we'll get him.

After that we came home. I got three letters. One from B and H, one from J in Belgium, and one from Pres. A.
