Today we were in Quedlingburg. We had a Termin with Herr B. When we got there he wasn't there, but we ate the little something anyhow, and waited for an hour before he showed up. We then just made an appointment for next week. We then visited a few other people, including the old lady that talks forever. All went well.

We left for home at 16:04. Then it was to home for a couple of minutes, then to Institute. We had a full house, all the normals and U, too. Then we came home and planned for Distrikt Versammlung.
