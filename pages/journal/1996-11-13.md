Today went pretty darn well. Mostly because it was P-day, but that's besides the point. I wrote a couple of letters, one home, one to K and one to P. I got one from home that said he's on the wrong path and not doing good. I wrote him a letter that will hopefully help him. We did our laundry today, too. I finally dried my clothes in a dryer for the first time in five months. It cost me 10DM! An outrage. Oh well, from now on it's free because a lady in the ward does our laundry.

At about 13:00 we went out and about. We visited a couple of cheesy churches. I bought a bell for mom at one. It sounds pretty good. Then we did a bit of Christmas shopping. I bought, finally, something for the family; a Räuchermann (incense smoker). It cost me 73DM. I also bought 30DM worth of stamps. To top it off, we also went shopping where I spent another 32DM. That really sucked. Shopping that is. We bought for two weeks, and tried to ride it all home on bikes. Elder B had the milk and juice on his rack. Halfway home all the juice fell off, luckily none busted open. When he came back to help me with my bag that broke, his bike fell over and then a couple of juices broke. We eventually got everything home.

Then it was more relaxing until 18:00. We made a few calls, and R called us. She's doing wonderful. She only had one cigarette today but she also declined when someone asked her if she wanted one. She'll make it!! Our appointments for tonight fell out, so we did TzT for an hour then came home. That's the day. I haven't been able to fix my bike yet, but I will soon. I hope.
