<pre id="letter">
Today has gone pretty well so far. There is not much of the day left, and nothing is planned, so I think it is safe to say the day is over. This morning was cool. I made pancakes for everybody. Then I went back to sleep for an few hours. Elder V and his comp Elder J went to a school class to tell them about Mormons. They said that went pretty well. At about 2:00 we all went to the Bahn Hoff to meet the two Sisters here in Braunschweig and the other Elders. Then we all went to a big park to play capture the flag. The park was about as big as Halberstadt. It's just like a big piece of the forest in the middle of the city. Capture the flag was a lot of fun. I got a bloody nose, because I ran through a bunch of little tree branches. I also had sore feet. I brought only my sandals, because I didn't think I would do any running or anything like that. It was a lot of fun, though. Our team won, of course. A neat thing is that Elder B L is in my Zone now. I saw him today, too. He ws on my team in capture the flag. He's doing pretty well. After that, most of the Elders played b-ball in the park. I couldn't in sandals, so I just slept. Then we came home, to Braunschweig, I mean. These past few days have been really weird. It's really different being in a big city. There is only one more day left here in Braunschweig though. Then it's back to Halberstadt and back to normal.
</pre>
