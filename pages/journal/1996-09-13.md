Today was Friday the 13th. Hopefully that isn't a bad sign. I'm worried, I fear, that all the good habits that I got started with Elder C are going to disappear. Elder N is really different from Elder C. He is more laid back and I get the impression that following the rules to the T isn't important to him. It is to me, and I think that is where problems will arise, if they do. I certainly hope they don't.

Things are a whole lot different now. I'm not sure if I like this change or not. Hopefully only good things will come.

I had a blast with Elder C. It's true I thought he was really annoying at first. But now that he's gone, I wish he was back. He was a great missionary, and a great friend. I hope Elder N and I can become good friends, too. I'm going to try my best!
