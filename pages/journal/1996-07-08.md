This day was different, but in a cool way. Elder C and I spent the morning in Quedlinburg. We visited Familie B, and we had another appointment that didn't show.

We got back from Quedlinburg about 13:30, and Elders J & V were already there. We all came home and had soup and Flavor-aid for lunch. Then we split and went to work. I went with Elder J. We first went to a bunch of old contacts that he could remember; he was in Halberstadt a year ago. Then we visited with Schwester B. We had planned to teach her a second discussion, but that didn't work out. After that we were going to visit another contact of Elder J's on the other side of town, but we met up with Elders C & V so we all went to get a Dönner and then came home.

I also got my pictures back. I'm mad, too. I had thought I took pictures and the MTC on that roll of film, but they weren't there, and there was a bunch of others missing. The negatives were even missing a few numbers. I don't think I'll go there again. I was also mad because a lot of my pictures didn't turn out as well as planned. Oh well!
