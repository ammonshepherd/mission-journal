Today went alright. We did a lot of TzT. That's mostly what we did. We finally met with W. A., too. He was an old man that I talked to on the street a few weeks ago. He lives in one of the big buildings on Borchardi Street. Not like that will mean anything to me in a few months, but oh well, where he lives I mean. Anyhow, we'll visit him in a few weeks.

We also met B's, a girl we met doing TzT, mother. We talked with her for a while about the exchange student they're getting from Idaho. She's LDS and coming in August. So we'll probably get to know her well, since she doesn't speak any German and it always works out that way anyhow. That's all for today.
