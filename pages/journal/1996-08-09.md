date: 1996-08-09
location: Halberstadt

---

Today went pretty well. This morning we mailed Elder H's packages and got an Abmelden form for Elder A. I also bought a new seat for my bike and a few other things too. I also got suspenders for Dad, they're German ones.

After that we had Mittags. Then we spent about an hour getting our bikes ready for the rest of the day. I put on the new seat and front light, and switched U-lock holders. Then we were off and riding through Halberstadt. First we visited a few contacts. Mostly from our newly received horde of TzT Berichts and Kontakts.

Then we did some TzT on Semmelweise Str. We saw Ashley so we stopped and talked with her. We're supposed to pick her up on Sunday Morning before Church if her house Mom can't give her a ride. We'll see what happens. Then we did some more TzT in the place where there is new stuff being built. We did that until 19:10, and then we were off to Brd. J's. There was quite a few people there. I ate my fill and had plenty Lemonade and grapefruit-ade to drink. Brd. John had a lot left over so he gave us a lemonade and two waters, and a whole bunch of Brötchen.

Today was a good hard work day. Oh yeah, I also sent a letter to Mom and Dad saying that I was ticked off that they didn't have faith in me. Now I feel a little bit sorry because it was kind of harsh.
