Wow. One day after the other. The days keep getting weirder and weirder. This morning we called I. She wasn't doing too much better, but she was still alive. She still didn't want to pray, and felt real bad. We also called Pres. to see what we should do. This morning was a pretty slacker morning. I didn't really study at all, because of what has been going on. Somehow time passed and we had omelets for breakfast, and after we planned for today and what not we left and went by on Kontakten and did some more TzT. We worked for a good while and then came home and ate lunch. After that we visited a member for a while and then caught the train to Bad Oldesloe. There I met M. He is a really cool guy. Very spiritually deep. Anyhow, we were supposed to teach S the second, but we couldn't get but a minute speaking time. M and S did all the talking. M mostly. He gave like a talk or something. It was really cool, full of examples, parables and what not.

After that we tried to catch the train ride home. The sign said to Lübeck, but the train went to Hamburg. Luckily we realized that and were able to get out at Bargteheide, where Elder R is at, and catch the train back to Bad Oldesloe, and then back to Lübeck. We then went home, and only an hour late because we missed, or took, the wrong train. I forgot we called I back before we left, and she said she had finally prayed. It was totally awesome. She felt the Spirit, and she's doing alright now. Pres. also called tonight and we told him everything was going well. Mission life is soo weird. I'm glad to be one though. I think I will still be baptized this Sunday. And she's quitting smoking, too. We talked to her again after we talked to Pres. and she went the whole day without smoking. She'll make it!
