Today went pretty well. I paid fast offerings instead of tithing this month, so that was a plus already.  Church went well.

All us Elders ate at Schwester B's today. Potatoes and meatloaf. I only had one helping of them, but two of the soup before. I didn't eat the zucchini salad this time and nobody forced more on me either. For Nachtisch we had sahne ice mit mehr sahne. The Germans love whipped cream!

After lunch we talked a bit and looked at Schwester M's pictures. She gave one to me that had me making a funny face in it. Everyone name me "the Missionary with many faces." Because all the pictures I'm in have a different and goofy face. Na ja.

After all that fun, Elder C and I went to visit Schwester B, but she wasn't home. The whole time walking there and stuff, I was feeling really dizzy and light headed. I also had to pee. So we came back and met the other Elders coming back to do the same thing. After that, I felt much better. I also prayed that I would feel better and i did. So I'll give the credit to Heavenly Father.

Then we visited Herr H and talked with him about his job. I just watched T.V. Then we came back and sang with Bruder J. I think we sounded good.

I then called Br. D to see when I could buy his bike. He had thought I didn't want to, so he fixed it up and was going to use it. But he'll still let me buy it for 200DM. Good deal, Great guy.
