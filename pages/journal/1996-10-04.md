All in all, today was a good day. We went by on a few contacts this morning. Two told us to come back later, and the other two weren't home. After that we came home and ate card board pizza. After Mittags we went to our appointment with Frau K. No one answered the first time we klingeled, so we waited for twenty minutes and tried again. That time she answered. She had been asleep. She talked to us about the problems with the people living underneath them, and other stuff, so we didn't get to anything spiritual. We ended up not spending time with the kids because Frau K had to pick them up but had no car. So there would have been no time. So then we came home and I did the contact list while Elder N prepared a lesson for home teaching. Then we went to our appointment with Fam. E. Only the son, D, was there because Herr E was stuck in Frankfurt on business. We ended up just talking with D about stuff. I still have hope that he has interest in hearing the discussions, but Elder N thinks differently. We'll see where it goes. After that we rushed to Sch. B. We were 15 minutes late. We had a feast of Brötchen. Sch M's friend also came. At the end we gave them the lesson, the cookies, and we walked Sch. M's friend home. 

That was all for today.
