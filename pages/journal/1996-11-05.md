Today was definitely different. Nothing too much happened until we had lunch with P's. Elder N did some stuff for Institute and I kept myself busy. We left on time for P's but ended up 10 minutes late. I had fun there, a good meal too. We ended up staying there too long so that we couldn't catch the train to Wernigerode and Darlingerode. So we came home and id stuff until Institute. That went o.k. We just watched the seminary BoM video. I had a lot of fun hanging around with J, U, B, A and Brd. J. They're really cool. I'll miss them. After Institute we came up and waited for someone to call. It came while I was writing home. I'm going to Lübeck. My comp is Elder B. I've heard a lot of good things about him. He's a hard worker and stuff. It will be really different from here.

Around 9:00 or so B and A came by and asked our help to move stuff from her garden over here. We did that. It went quick.

Today is pretty weird. The day is finally come that I leave Halberstadt. I've had a lot of good, bad, awesome, terrible experiences here. I'll surely miss this place and all the people here. It was like a home.
