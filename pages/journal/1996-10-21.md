The ups and downs of today, when meshed together, in an attempt to reach a one word description of said day, would come out as o.k.

The weather, for this the first-and-twenty day of the month of October, in the day ouf our Lord one thousand nine hundred and six and ninety, was, shall I say, dismal. The rain persisted in falling, making everything wet. My wax-jacket, of which I am very happy of, held up to the we weather rather well. The inside staying dry, while the outside sustaining only a minor dampness. It brings me immense pleasure to own a jacket like that.

The activities of this day were relatively few and nun-too-notable. This morning, before visiting Frau F at 11:00, I took apart the big white closets in the apartment downstairs. It was a job that brought seemingly quam amusement, but was needed nonetheless to be done.

After visiting with Frau F, and vacuuming her floor, we came home and attempted to prepare something edible for our mid-day meal. My mind was not securely made up as to what I would make. Because of this blunder, the concoction, I will refer to as STUFF, was anything and everything BUT desirable for consumption. It was a wrongful mixture of soups an fish from a can. The smell alone was enough to make a garbage fly dizzy. After a lot of spices were pointlessly added to the metal eating, grimy STUFF, it tasted one point better on a scale from 1 to 100, where it was at 99, being most foul. After this degenerative form of dinner at lunch time, we visited the long time investigator Frau K. We stayed but one hour, do to the fact that Frau K had to pick up D from someplace. We talked only a small portion about things pertaining to a religious matter. We made a further appointment for this Friday. The time left between appointment with Frau K and the appointment for evening bread with the family P was spent, by me, in furthering the progress of putting the old door to door information onto the new super door to door Berichts.

We left with plenty of time for us to reach P's in good time. We met the two Drunk Brothers on the way. We helped them carry their bag of booze for a while, and then wen to P's. I had a rather enjoyable time. With good food and friends, that is easily achieved. My joy lasted until Elder N, for no reason, except that sometimes he is a degenerate form of a brainless monkey, ridiculed the Mesa Temple once again. That makes me quite perturbed when he does that. I sometimes (most of the time) receive the impression that Elder N thinks he is in someway "better" than others, mostly me. If I'm wrong, good! But that is the impression I receive.

I learn lots of things while being on a mission. I see in people good and bad things. The good things I try to emulate; the bad things I try to remind myself, when tempted to do them, of how I feel when they were done to me, or when I saw them. With that in mind, I hope to become more like the Perfect One, my Lord, Jesus!
