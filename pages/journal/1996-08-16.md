Today went o.k. and also pretty fast. This morning we had a district meeting and I gave the theme. It was no problem because it was only Elder C and me. That went all fine and dandy. After that we visited W. A. and then went to some Zurückkehrens on the far north side of Halberstadt. Then we came home at 14:00 and had Mittags. Then it was back to work. We did a whole lot more Zurückkehrens, and also a little bit of TzT. We went by foot today, so mine really hurt. Just a couple of days riding a bike will take the stuff that makes it easy to walk all day, right out of you. Nothing exciting happened today. That's probably for the better.
