Today was o.k. We had six appointments, so there wasn't too much to plan for. Our first one fell out though, so we took our packages to Post and got those mailed off. Then we went looking for a picture frame for R's present. We found a normal one, but not a folding one. S we'll just put the mirror and picture in the same frame. After that we did some street contacting until our appointment with Sch H. That went well. She had good food and I went away totally stuffed. We helped her take some trash stuff out to a garden, and then Bishop picked us up and took us to our appointment with Frau W. That was pretty dumb. She likes to talk and doesn't even want to be baptized. We have to go back anyhow, so we'll see how it goes. After that we all went to R's and taught her the 6th discussion. It was cool. Bishop was a lot of help. That went pretty well. After that Bishop dropped us off by our other appointment. 

The guy didn't have interest after all. Then we visited a Russian family and made an appointment to teach the first. Then we visited D. He, surprisingly, let us in and we had a nice talk with him. Elder B was awesome and turned a dead end situation into something. We made another appointment with him, and now he's interested. After that we went to our appointment with L. The lady is so golden, but the man is a total Atheist and a jerk about it, too. He's stuck in his ways and he won't let his wife take the discussions or even let us talk about the Church with her. It's sad to see. We talked to R tonight and we think she has a concern about abortion. Hopefully she will be able to pass the interview.
