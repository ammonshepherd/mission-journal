Today was a good day. Church went well. I did OK with my talk. After church we had Mittags by Ps. After that we came home and took a little nap and did some work on the contact list and the like. Then we rushed over to Sch. B's. We stayed there for a while then rushed back home so we could sing with Brd. J. We were too late, though, coming back and met Brd. J as he was going home. He was a little upset that he had to wait and that we didn't show. He forgave us and we will sing on Thursday morning now. That's all for today!
