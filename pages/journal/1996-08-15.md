Today was a nice day, weather wise. It rained a little bit in the Abend, but the morning until afternoon was clear skies and sun. We mostly tried to reach old contacts today. We did have an appointment with Herr R. I hope something good will come out of that. He's already had all the discussions, so we are going slowly through them again. After that we tried to contact a few more Zurückkehrens. We did that for a while then came home and went to the bathroom, and then went back out to work. We tried to contact a few more people, but no one was home. We did that until 8:30pm. It was cool because by then it was getting dark because of the clouds. So by the time to be outside in the dark for 2 months. the last time was at the MTC. Oh well, nothing exciting happened today either.
