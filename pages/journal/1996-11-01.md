date: 1996-11-01

Today went alright. We didn't leave until late today. Around 12:00. When we finally did go, we bought Monatskarte and went to Post. I finally mailed my letter, and also all the letters I've been saving up. I wanted to send home something for Christmas, but I didn't have anything to send, and no money to buy anything. After that we came home and threw something down our throats while we waited for J to come. We walked to the Wärmstube because J rode his bike. There we found out that they won't need help for a long while. We'll have to wait until the call us. AFter that we came back and J used the phone and then left. Elder N and I went by Herr R and luckily he was home. We had a nice talk with him and made out a new appointment. After that we went to Sch. F's. Since we were later than she had expected, she put the fruit she had prepared for us away. She was happy to see us anyhow, and we still got the fruit. She also made us Abendbrot. We sat and talked with her for an hour or so. AFter F's, we wanted to visit Herr K, but right out from F's we met Herr B (Brown Mantel Man). We stood out in the cold, listening to him talk about all kinds of stuff for 1 1/2 hours. He talked like a crazy man this time. Something about his vision and two spaceships and that God is a commander of one or something like that. It was pretty weird, like usual. After that long talk we came home, and my feet were pretty much thawed out by the time we got home.
