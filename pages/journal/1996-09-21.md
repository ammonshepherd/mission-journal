Today went pretty well. I got all the things studied that needed to, and I also did pushups and crunches. I'm still feeling a bit sick, lots of coughing and an eternal fountain of snot, which is my nose. We got to Bruder J's only 15 minutes late. We read 4 Nephi with him. I also took him a thing of cookies. Today was COLD and RAINY. It rained the whole day and was about 6°C. After Bruder J we came home and I made Kräuter creme Suppe with rice. It tasted pretty good. It would have tasted better if I hadn't receive the letter I did. Today I found out that I'm no longer a member of the 14th ward, but because of boundary changes, our house is now in 27th ward boundaries. It was a great shock to me. I don't like it one bit. Now when I get home, everything is going to be totally different! Na, ja. Life SUCKS!

After Mittags we visited H for an hour or two, then made an appointment with Herr E, and then went by Frau K. She told us she would call us. In other words, she's not interested. Herr K and Schwester B were both not home. 

That was pretty much all that happened. It was rainy, cold, and gray! I'm not looking forward to going home, now, because everything will be changed. I HATE CHANGE!
