title: "The heights of supremeness"
date: 1996-10-24
location: Halberstadt
template: post
---

Today started out in the depths of the toilet and ended up in the heights of supremeness. We left the apartment at halb neun (8:30) to go to Brd. J's old apartment and help him move out. There were a lot of workers already there, so we ended up just sitting around. We ended up walking back to the church with Brd. J as the last load was taken by truck. We then helped him move the junk up.

After that, we came up and had a long Mittags pause. We both wrote letters because we had had not time to write yesterday. I was proud of myself. I finally wrote DC (my first companion) and wrote back MH. I also wrote S and home.

After that, we actually put on our suits for the almost the first time in three days. We went by on a few contacts. We got tschüßed by one, and actually got let in by Herr L. After that we took the tupperware by D's. They weren't home, but A was babysitting. She made us come in and stay for a little bit.

After that we came back and had Abendbrot with Brd. J and B. That was a whole ton of fun. I got a cool had from Brd. J. I also helped B by translating a thing for her.

Today was downright cold. Starting at 3°C and not going higher than 10° the whole day. My coat kept me warm.

I think I'll miss Halberstadt when I finally go. I've had a ton of fun here.
