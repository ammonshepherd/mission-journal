I received no letters today. But I did get reimbursements. I'll start from the beginning of today, which went by ok.

This morning I slept until 6:40. My body needed it, so it took the rest. After that study time went as normal. I just need to polish up the second, and then I can pass it off. After all the studying I started on the cookies. I made cookies until 12:00, and then made macaroni and cheese for lunch. this batch of macaroni and cheese was almost the worst ever. The first one was the worst, because it had no taste. This one tasted ok, but had chunks of flour and cheese and just a nasty texture to it. It still turned out really pasty, too. By the last few fork fulls I thought I would throw up. One of these days I'll get it right. I didn't change into my suit until 13:00! After lunch, we went by Post and I wanted to cash my check, but the Bank wasn't even open. Then we went by on Sch. B. We ended up staying there for an hour and a half. We had some cake and stuff from P's birthday. He showed up for an half-hour and then went back out. I really feel sorry for that family. It's really messed up.

After B's we went to Sch. M. We didn't call because we were afraid she would tell us not to come, so we just showed up. We had a good long visit with her. We sang a few songs (hymns) to cheer her up and she read her favorite verses from the Book of Mormon. AFter two hours at M's we decided to head back home because it was almost 21:30. On the way home I got a picture from the crosswalk signal. The one with the guy with the walking stick, cap, and backpack. I hope it turns out. All in all, today went by pretty good.

    I still can't make mac & cheese from scratch. The picture did not turn out. 2026 Internet could only find one image that looks pretty close to what I remember.
