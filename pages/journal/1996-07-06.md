On this day, we spent some time in Quedlinburg. First off, we spent some time figuring out what to do for Primary. Then we tried to do some street contacting. We did that for about an hour and a half, with not much luck. I made Knödel, rice, green beans, and gravy for lunch, with cool-aid. Then we did some more street contacting until the train left for Quedlinburg. In Quedlinburg we spent 2 hours listening to Frau B. She's the real old lady that gives kisses. A funny thing that happened there was she gave us a cup of water. My cup was filthy and the water was brown because there was dirt in it. I didn't want to drink it, so I poured it in a pot on the stove behind me. After that we did some Tür zu Tür and made a few appointments. Then we cam home. I did the dishes and prepared a cheesy lesson for primary.
