Today went really well for me. Not so well for Elder N. This morning I did well with studying and everything. Elder N was running late, and so we didn't get out until about 11:00. We went by on Kontakts until about 13:00. Then we came home for lunch. After an o.k. lunch we started cleaning out the downstairs. We brought a few things up, but there is still a whole lot left to do. We did that until 17:00, when Erntedankfest started. That went pretty well. I had fun playing with the kids afterwards. There was American Jello, too. And lots of other good things to eat. Familie K showed up about 45 minutes late. I was glad that they came. Sch. B & C came after the program and during the feeding time. A had a friend that came, too. A tall, and I think cute (not that that matters) girl from somewhere in Southern Germany. She was pretty cool.

Today was my first day with my wax-jacket. I love it! Elder N's pretty much fell apart on him, so he's going to take it back. I'm so happy that I bought it. I also got a letter from Dad. I think that's one every day this week.

Brd. J moving in is giving Elder N an ulcer. I hope it all will go quickly and painlessly. I hope K and B will come to church. I need to finish the 2nd Discussion this month. That's all for today.
