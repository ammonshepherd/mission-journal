Today started out OK, but I really turned it into a cruddy end. We all, Elder L & J & N & I went to the Wernigerode castle. Elder N got a real bloody nose while up there. It was pretty funny. After that Elder L and J went home and Elder N and I went by on a member that lives in Wernigerode. She was there, but didn't let us in. After that we came home. I did buy and Astrix and Obelix comic book in the Wernigerode Bahnhof. They are really cool. I'm going to start collecting them.

I made a quick soup after I cleaned up the kitchen. After that we had planned on going real quick by Familie K and then to Brd J's. We ended up staying at K's until 11:30pm. I didn't like staying there at all. When we got home (we got a ride from K) I tried to tell Elder N that I didn't like staying out that late, but it all came out wrong and I ended up causing contention, BIG TIME. In the course of sticking my foot in my mouth, I insulted Elder N by insinuating that he doesn't do anything right, or that he's doing it all bad. I kind of fixed what I did, but he probably thinks I'm a real idiot now. I really like to screw things up. Sometimes I wish that I didn't care about the rules so that I would get along with most of the other missionaries. They always say that you've got to be yourself. They insinuate that that means following the rules are bad. I know that we can be ourselves and follow the rules, too. It's called bettering ourselves.

Oh well! I'm not perfect, I'm just trying unsuccessfully to be.
