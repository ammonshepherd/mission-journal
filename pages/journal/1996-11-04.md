Today was almost completely a waste of time. Unfortunately, that has happened a lot since Elder N got here.

Before our weekly trip to Frau F's we dropped Elder N's suit off at the cleaners, and then went to the bank. Up until that time the day was going beautifully. The bank ruined it because they decided to be JERKS! They wouldn't let me cash my check, the dimwits! They gave some dumb excuse and that was that. We then helped out Frau F and afterwards came home for lunch. We called Pres. to see what we could do, but he (didn't) couldn't do anything. I did make a good lunch, though. Noodles with some kind of sauce, with Halberstädter Würstchen.

After lunch is when we wasted time. We spent 2 hours looking for the number for Darlingerode, the Behindert kid that lives there; and trying to figure out what to do for the rest of the day. That's another thing I don't like, we never plan.

We finally went out and went by on some contacts. We got a hold of two. Both of which said not to come back. We also visited Sch. B. She got back from the hospital last week. She broke her elbow pretty good, but she's a tuff one and she's doing alright. After that we came home.

Today wasn't a total loss, but it could have been way better. I did get a letter from Mom & Dad and K. I even got $20 from Mom and Dad.

I'm pretty sure I'll get transferred. I'll find out tomorrow. I'll be sad and happy for a lot of different reasons when I finally go.
