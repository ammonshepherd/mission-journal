Today went OK. Long and boring for a P-day, but that's how they are in Halberstadt. I was really, really lazy this morning, and didn't get out of bed until 8:00. Elder N got out of bed then too, and while I read my Book of Mormon he got everything ready for the phone guy to come. We had been told that he would come vormittags. He didn't come until 12:30. I took a shower/bath and started writing letters while waiting for the phone guy. It takes me forever to write letters, so I don't write a lot. Today I wrote home, to K, HP, and to MC. I sent his name tag back, too. After the phone guy left, by the way, it's all fixed and we just have to wait for a week for the power to come on, anyhow, after he left we took our letters to Post and then went to Kaufland, where Elder N bought some CDs. After that we went weight lifting. A came too. I didn't work myself too hard, but just enough I figure. After that we came home and I read in the Ensign until English Class. That went o.k. Normal I should say. After English class we had planned to visit Sch. B, but we went as far as the music store and decided to come back. We would have been back about 10:00 or 10:30 if we had visited them. We came back instead and Elder N called to see how everything was going. The little girl, I can never remember her name, had broken her arm. That's why we wanted to visit today. Anyhow, we'll see them Friday and/or Saturday.

That's all for today.
