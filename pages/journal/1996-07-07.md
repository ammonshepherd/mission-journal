This day was OK at first. Church and all went fine. In primary, it was just the K's, C & M. For the first hour we just messed around and played the organ. Then I tried to give my lesson. About halfway I just read the stories and ended like that. Then Elder C did a little something, and then we played hangman for 15 minutes or so until it was time to go.

After church, we had lunch at the P's. The food there was all right. Nothing to gag over. After a while there, we got a ride back from J. We then rested for a little while, then headed out to do street contacting.

We ended up seeing B, about 10 minutes after we left the house. She gave us a ride to Blankenburg; the guy wasn't there. Then she took us to her cousin's and then we came back to practice a song with Bruder J.

After Bruder J left, B was still here. She then went and bought pizza for her and Elder C, they ate in the basement alone. Dang, I wish he wouldn't allow that to happen. We talked a little about our problem with B today. I at least know now that he thinks it is a problem, too. We're trying to think of a way to tell B we need to stop hanging around her, but to do it in a nice way. I don't think there is. Well, I hope it all turns out good. Nothing that is right is ever easy. Doch!
