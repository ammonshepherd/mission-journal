Today we took our P-Day because yesterday was Zone Conference. We went shopping today, and only spent 30DM for the two weeks. That's cool. Hopefully we will have enough food to last. I cleaned the bathroom after we finished shopping, and then started writing letters. I wrote one home and one to B, and sent them in the same envelope. I also wrote to M H. Now I just need tow rite to Ja nd D and Elder F before the year ands. I also want to write to S, T, and J, and a few other Elders that I have addresses for We'll see how long it takes for me to write them.

We had an awesome Mittags today. We made hobo dinners. Made with potatoes, carrots, onions, and I had tons of sausage and Elder C had hamburger meat. I had a big heaping plate full. It tasted wonderful! I'll have to do that again sometime. After Mittags and a little bit of relaxing, we visited the tower that can be seen on Spieglsberg. It was a lot funner doing the mountain biking than seeing the tower. I went cruising down a couple steep trails. It was a total blast. I'd like to ride those trails again sometime.

Anyhow. After that fun we had an hour to relax before 18:00 and working time. I tried to study German, but slept instead. 

We visited Familie K tonight. I sure hope that they will keep interest, They seem like a cool familie. The mom, dad, a boy and a girl. They would make great members. We'll (start) keep working on them.
