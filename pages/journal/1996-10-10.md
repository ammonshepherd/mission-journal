Today went pretty well. This morning I sent off two Kinder Eir for Hope. I also found out that I was a day late to cash my check for reimbursements. Then we went to Bahnhof and picked up Elder L and J. We all came home and I made Knödel and Würstchen, with vegetables too. After that Elder J and I went by on a whole bunch of contacts. Almost all of them were not home, and those that were had no time right then. We also went by on P's and D's. They were both not home. So that was my first experience where I was the one who knew the most German. It was pretty cool. Stuff like that goes to my head, but I didn't let me turn stupid. We, Elder J and I, came home about 19:00. Just after we got home Brd. J came by. I talked to him for a while and showed him his apartment. After that we came back up and Elder N and L came back. That was pretty much the whole day.

I think it's interesting how some missionaries try to follow the rules. And because they try other missionaries make fun of them, think they are too tight, and all sorts of dumb stuff. Those that don't follow rules and stuff, at least I get the impression, that they think they are cool because they don't follow the rules. I think they're dumb because they're screwing up their salvation. Oh well, they can think what they want and screw up their lives. I can't do anything and they won't listen to me anyhow.
