title: "Dancing on Oscar Meier's grave"
date: 1996-07-18
location: Halberstadt
template: post
---

Today was my first experience with a German funeral. It was quite different. Us four Elders were invited to go. There was no services, just the burial; that was in Quedlinburg. I feel kind of bad, because before the thing started, before we walked to the grave, we all were cracking jokes and laughing. Then, even worse, on the way to the grave site Elder H and I saw a grave for Oscar Meier. We almost started busting up laughing. It was terrible. Everything else went pretty good.

After that, Elder C and I did some door to door on Johannes-Sebastian-Back Straße. It was a normal street, nobody was interested. We did that until 14:03 when the Zug (train) came. Then we got Elder C Ammelded (registered), so no he's legal in Halberstadt. After that we came home for an hour and wrote our talks for Zone Conference. Then we went to our appointment with Familie S. Frau P wasn't there, but we had a nice meal and a nice talk. Like the most people, they had no further interest, so that was a bummer. By the time we were done, the Frau P showed up so we had a quick talk with her. She still has interest and we made an appointment for Sunday and read 3 Nephi 11. After that we came home and got everything ready for Zone Conference.
