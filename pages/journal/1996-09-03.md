This morning we were in Quedlinburg. We met with Herr B and read with him in the Book of Mormon. Then we pretty much walked around, we did make an appointment with a lady for Tuesday in two weeks.

After that we came back to Halberstadt and checked in to see if we could get a permit to have an Ausstellung in the Altestadt Fest. We ran around for an hour finding the place and when we did, it was closed for lunch. So we came home and had ours, lunch that is. We also took time for Elder C to prepare for Institute.

Institute itself went pretty well. At the end B was looking at my brochures for the Lektionen and read that Jesus had created the earth. She didn't believe that, so I tried to find scriptures right then to help her out. I couldn't find any though. After that we checked on a few contacts and then bought croques and came home and ate them. Croques are like sub-sandwiches, but better. That was all for today.

I've started a list of scriptures for B that talk about Christ creating the world. Hopefully they will help her understand better.

Also today I got a letter from K. I was surprised that it wasn't a Dear John. It's too bad I don't know if she really likes me. I wish shd did. Oh well, I don't need to worry about that now. If she is still "free" when I get home, I'll take her out and maybe marry her.
