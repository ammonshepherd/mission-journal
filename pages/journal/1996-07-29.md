Today went pretty quick. We had a few appointments and they were spread out throughout the day so with traveling time and doing street contacting on the way, everything went by fast. We sang with Bruder J again today. Today was really short because he had to go somewhere else. 

Then we had an appointment fall out, but we also made a new one. We also had a guy threaten to call the police on us. He also had us scratch out his name on our list. I thought it was kind of funny. Some old guy making a big scene because we wrote down his name. It was kind of stupid if you think about it. Na ja. We're here to oblige. We scratched out his name and went on our merry way.
