Sometimes LIFE SUCKS! Today was pretty much like a wasted day. We spent the morning finishing up R's present, and we also visited M at his work. He makes false teeth and stuff. We visited him for a while and he's letting us make fangs for ourselves. We got them started today and we'll finish them on Wednesday.

After that we went to the church for the baptismal interview for R. While she was in that, Elder B started on the pizzas. I helped and also played cards with the youth. UNFORTUNATELY, R did NOT pass the interview. We had to wait until later tonight to find out for sure from Pres. B. So it was pretty tense waiting. The pizza party was fun, though. I got to know the youth a bit better. I played mostly UNO. The pizza was good. I ate a red chilly pepper, and it was HOT! Elders R and C came with the exchange student and had some fun there. Elders W and W were also there. They are staying the night, because R was supposed to be baptized tomorrow. Elder W helped find her, and Pres. gave him permission to come here for that. Unfortunately, it didn't work out. Elder C and R and the girl went home that night, and Elders W and W stayed here. We didn't find out until we got home that R would have to wait.
