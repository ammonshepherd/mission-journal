Today went pretty darn well. We had a nice discussion with Herr R. about prophets. After that we went to Frau F for service. I noticed today that she had Halberstädter glasses. I asked her if she knew where I could buy some, and she simply gave them to me. It was awesome and very nice. She just up and gave them to me. After Frau F we took the cups and the ice home and then went to meet with Frau K. Another prayer was answered and Herr K was also home. We talked with them for two hours about stuff. No gospel theme, just talking and getting to know each other. I had no problems with that, but we, or Frau K, talked to us for two hours! After that we came home and ate a quick Mittags and then left to do TzT. We did that until our termin with Familie B. They ended up both not being there. Frau B was on vacation with her mom, and Herr B had gotten called to work the evening shift. We did see him and talk to him on our way to check zurückkehrens.

So after our fallen out appointment, we walked through half the city doing the above said zurückkehrens. The sky was nice and full with big dark clouds, and it rained pretty good too. We did the zurückkehrens until 8:30pm and then came home. My feet are very, very sore.
