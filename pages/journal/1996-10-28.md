Today was pretty darn good. This morning we went by on Herr PR with no luck. After that we helped out Frau F. After that we took our Mittags Pause.

Then we went and bought us some food! We bought enough to last us until Wed. because then Brd. P insists on taking us. Now we actually have food in our house.

After that we actually did what we had planned and visited Sch. B in Blankenburg Hospital. After that we started walking to Heimburg, 5km away, to visit our Empfehlung. We walked to Oesig where we were helped by a nice bus driver to the bus we needed to Heimburg. We looked for the street and everything, bu there wasn't even the name of that street in Heimburg. Well, after that we came home. We rode the bus to Blankenburg and then the train to Halberstadt. We got out at the Spiegelsberge stop so it only took 5 minutes to walk home. It was pretty cool.

The train ride there was really pretty, mainly because it was different. We didn't get ahold of the guy in Heimburg, but I sure felt like I was actually doing something today.

I'm running low on money this month because I bought that jacket. I think it will eventually be worth it.

I don't know what exactly to say, but today just felt really good.

I also talked with Brd. j for a while. Elder N and I went down and kept him company for a while. I think he was really happy to see us tonight.

I don't know, but I think I'll miss this place when I finally go.
