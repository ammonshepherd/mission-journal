Today went really well. Today was fast Sunday because last Sunday was General Conference. Normally I start after Mittags on Sat, but because we had Abendbrot at B's, I couldn't do that. So I"m fasting from after Mittags today until Mittags tomorrow, Monday. Church went well. I think everybody that was there today bore their testimonies. There were a few less people than normal. I played all the songs in Sacrament because Sch. Z and Sch. D were both not there. Luckily only three hymns.

After church, we had Mittags a P's. Brd P was sick and didn't come to Church, but was doing better when we came for Mittags. Right after P's we went to Familie K. None of them were in Church today. Anyhow, we all went on a ride to a place right by Blankenburg. We first visited the dam there. It was pretty cool. After that we went to Hermannshöle. It was a neat little cave. I really had a lot of fun with the K. I'll be really sad if they don't eventually join the church. The mom is really ready. The dad needs a little work and mostly time. It'll be hard because they both like to smoke a lot, and he likes to drink a lot too. I'm partly fasting that they will be able to receive the help they need to be able to become members and receive the joy and salvation that com from this true church.
