Today was a pretty good day. We worked the first part of the day in Quedlinburg. Our appointment with A fell out. We were walking by K's and they were just coming back from school, the two little ones and Sister K. She invited us for lunch, so we had lunch at the K's. We played with the two, M and C for about a 1/2 hour until lunch was ready. After all that fun we did some TzT on Johann-Sebastian-Bach Straße. We did have a little bit of luck in that some people, only a few, said that we could come back. When we came back to Halberstadt, we went by a Frau R, but she wasn't home. We then came home and got the Kontakt list. Then we did them for a few hours. We ended up by the Halberstadt cemetery. We walked through to find the oldest tombstone. The oldest one we could read was 1860's or somewhere in that area. After all that we came home.

We found out today that one of the apartments are going to be let go. Hopefully we can keep ours, the upper one. All that we need is a working phone line.

That's all for today.
