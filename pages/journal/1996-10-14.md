Today went o.k. Mostly a pretty relaxed day. We visited with Frau P. We read Alma 32 with her. I think she felt the Spirit, but that she is too scared or whatever to recognize it. After that we went by Frau F. We went in her basement today, and Elder N fixed her wood pile. I would have helped, but there was no room so I just watched. After that we went to the bank. Elder N cashed his check and I tried to cash mine again. We told them to call Altona where we have our main Konto. She still wouldn't do it so we came home. I successfully completed my fast, and then made tomato soup with noodles for lunch. Unfortunately, not enough. After Mittags we went by on a few contacts. We actually reached a few, unfortunately, they don't have any more interest. After that, we came home and I did some work on the TzT Berichts. I also took time to start mu talk for Zone Conference. I have a feeling that I'll be giving one this time.

I also read the little pamphlet called The Holy Temple by Boyd K. Packer. It really got me wishing that I could go to the temple. That is one of my favorite things to do. To go to the temple and relax. Let the cares of the world just slide away and enjoy the beauty.
