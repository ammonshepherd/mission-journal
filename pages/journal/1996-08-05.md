Today went pretty well and sehr schnell!

We did Tür zu Tür for the meisten Zeit (most of the time). Wir haben Tür zu Tür für die meisten Zeit gemacht. 

I knew I could write it in German.

Anyhow, in A's letter, he asked for stories. A few happened today, I think, so I'll write them quick so I don't forget.

We met a lady from New Zealand. I talked with her in German at first. She replied in English. I thought she was a German practicing English, so I said, "You speak very good English." She said, "That's because I am." I felt pretty dumb. She had kein Interesse anyway. So that was a funny little incident.

I'll tell him the dog story by F. S, and when we did charades and magic tricks for an hour with P B's friend. There's not really anything really neat to tell.

We also went to Bruder J's birthday party. Schwester P brought Küchen that she made. It tasted very good. We, all us Elders, were there for a little while. We sang Herrliches Zion and another one of Bruder J's favorite songs. We sounded cool.

After that we had a Termin, but we met the guy on the way there and we changed it to next we because today wasn't good.

I buy a bike tomorrow!!!!!
