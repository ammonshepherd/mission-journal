Today is actually here. My last day in Halberstadt. I'm actually writing this entry in tomorrow on the train to Lübeck.

Anyhow, Wed. was pretty action packed. I woke up jumped in the shower, ate, and then started packing. I thought it would take me the whole day, but only took an hour.

After that, at about 8:40 or so, we left to get our hairs cut. I got my cut really short this time. I like it. After we got our hairs cut, we went to Wernigerode to cash my check. Luckily they did it with no problem. We got to look around the Fußgänger Zone for a while, too. It was cool. After that we came home and had Mittags. Right after that we took my bike to the post office. That all went well, no problem. Then we tried to meld mich ab*, but the Rathaus (City Hall) isn't even open on Wed. So we just came home and relaxed until 18:00 when we had Abendbrot with Brd. J. I made some root beer, and it was hilarious to watch Brd. J drink it.

Then we suffered through another English class. After that we went to D's. To my surprise, the whole gang was there. We sat around for a while and then watched Elder N's family reunion video. It was good, it made me miss home.

I also gave Mom's book and tape to D's. They loved it. M and T fell asleep listening to it. They also said that they will call home or go over, when they are in Mesa for Thanksgiving and Christmas. That'll be cool. 

I didn't get to sleep until 12:00. I'm bushed.

    * In Germany at that time, you had to register yourself at city hall. They keep a record of when you move in and when you move out. Very German.
