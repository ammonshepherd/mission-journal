Germany Hamburg, April 10, 1996 - April 4, 1998
Home Journal

I'm transcribing my mission journal on the day it happened 30 years later.

I wrote in my journal every day on my mission. Well, to be more accurate, if I missed a day, I wrote about it the next day. Every day was accounted for.

Why? I wanted to write a very basic Python static site generator for a while, and since I started my mission 30 years ago, I thought this was a perfect excuse to do that. So I made pysite.

I left out full names and sometimes use a different letter to represent someone's real name. Just in case. I also fixed most grammar issues. My spelling was atrocious. I left most of the mistakes in German grammar and spelling in place, because it's cute, like a little kid learning to talk. And I thought it would be cool to see the progression as I got better. Sometimes I'll add some present day commentary to the bottom of the post.

An absolute real church building

November 12, 1996

Another day comes to an end. This one with mixed feelings. First of all, I'm sick of coming home late like we have the past 2 times and tonight. Because then we have to plan in the morning like we did today. Elder B hates it too, so we should have no problem.

We planned the baptism at I's. She doesn't know too many people, so I got asked to give her the gift of the Holy Ghost. I feel it an honor to do, but I'm pretty darn scared to do it. I've never done that before, let alone in a foreign language that I can hardly speak.

After I's and K's we had PFK. I finally got to see the church building. It's awesome. An absolute real church building.

After that we met with Herr B. He's a real character. He'll be baptized eventually. He just needs to trust his feelings. Today had a few ups and downs. I think I's holding back something. I sure hope she makes it. She will!
