Mission Journal

Germany Hamburg, April 10, 1996 - April 4, 1998
Home Journal

I'm transcribing my mission journal on the day it happened 30 years later.

I wrote in my journal every day on my mission. Well, to be more accurate, if I missed a day, I wrote about it the next day. Every day was accounted for.

Why? I wanted to write a very basic Python static site generator for a while, and since I started my mission 30 years ago, I thought this was a perfect excuse to do that. So I made pysite.

I left out full names and sometimes use a different letter to represent someone's real name. Just in case. I also fixed most grammar issues. My spelling was atrocious. I left most of the mistakes in German grammar and spelling in place, because it's cute, like a little kid learning to talk. And I thought it would be cool to see the progression as I got better. Sometimes I'll add some present day commentary to the bottom of the post.

General Conference, day 2!

October 06, 1996

Today was pretty awesome. Mainly because of Conference. We had the Priesthood session first, at 11:00. And after that we had about an hour or two until the next session, which was the Sat. afternoon one. We had lunch in between. Then the last one started at 18:00. It was the "live" Sunday morning one. It was a good Conference. My bum hurt for sitting for about 7 hours. We ended up not going with B, J and Brd. J because they didn't stay for the last one and we wanted to. B was really mad at us because of that. She had also come back because she "forgot something" and then wanted us to go back with her. I am glad that Elder N held strong to at least the rule that we aren't allowed to ride alone with girls. After that B was really mad. But on well. Rules are Rules! We stayed the night in Braunschweig with Elder V and J.

Also, at conference one of the members, an Italian kid (my age) started talking to me and asked me if we could be friends. It was kind of weird, or rather he was. He said he didn't have any friends. I said alright and gave him my address and he gave me his. Na ja.

Anyhow, that's about all for today.
