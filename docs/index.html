Germany Hamburg, April 10, 1996 - April 4, 1998
Home Journal

I'm transcribing my mission journal on the day it happened 30 years later.

I wrote in my journal every day on my mission. Well, to be more accurate, if I missed a day, I wrote about it the next day. Every day was accounted for.

Why? I wanted to write a very basic Python static site generator for a while, and since I started my mission 30 years ago, I thought this was a perfect excuse to do that. So I made pysite.

I left out full names and sometimes use a different letter to represent someone's real name. Just in case. I also fixed most grammar issues. My spelling was atrocious. I left most of the mistakes in German grammar and spelling in place, because it's cute, like a little kid learning to talk. And I thought it would be cool to see the progression as I got better. Sometimes I'll add some present day commentary to the bottom of the post.

Mexican food!

October 27, 1996

Today starte out really great. Elder N and I totally forgot that today the clocks were to be set an hour back. Without that knowledge, we went to GML at 8:00am and waited, but nobody came. We sang hymns until what we thought was 9:00am. Then came Brd J. He explained to us our mistake. AFter that everything went normally. J was finally released as a stake missionary after 6 years as one, and was pin in as GML (ward mission leader). Poor guy. 20 years old with the responsibilities of a 30 year old.* Elder N gave his talk and went over by 15 minutes. He had to cut a lot out to do that, too. He could have gone for another 30 or 40 minutes!

We had lunch at D's. Chicken enchiladas, and burritos, and chips and dip! Wow!! Needless to say, it was absolutely delicious! We stayed there for 3 hours, then went to P's. We were there for a while and then got a ride with J, B and U to Sch. B's. They stayed for a minute or two and then left. We stayed and read in the BoM and had a really good talk.

After that, we came home and sang with Brd. J. It was not nearly as good as with C, but oh well.

I got a tea remedy from Sch B that will hopefully get rid of the rash on my throat.

I forgot to give Mom's tape and songbook to D's. Maybe during the week sometime. At least before I go.

Today went well!

*I was ward mission leader after my mission at age 22 until I got married at age 23.
