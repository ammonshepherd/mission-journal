Germany Hamburg, April 10, 1996 - April 4, 1998
Home Journal

I'm transcribing my mission journal on the day it happened 30 years later.

I wrote in my journal every day on my mission. Well, to be more accurate, if I missed a day, I wrote about it the next day. Every day was accounted for.

Why? I wanted to write a very basic Python static site generator for a while, and since I started my mission 30 years ago, I thought this was a perfect excuse to do that. So I made pysite.

I left out full names and sometimes use a different letter to represent someone's real name. Just in case. I also fixed most grammar issues. My spelling was atrocious. I left most of the mistakes in German grammar and spelling in place, because it's cute, like a little kid learning to talk. And I thought it would be cool to see the progression as I got better. Sometimes I'll add some present day commentary to the bottom of the post.

All in all, a good day.

October 25, 1996

Today was pretty dang OK! This morning we went to the opening of the Wärmstube. It was quite interesting, but at the same time very boring. We sat for two hours listening to a lot of different people talk. What I found cool was the zwischen music. They had a recorder group, three, and for one thing they read a poem and played the guitar to it. The guitar parts were totally awesome. It sounded real cool because it was in one of those big churches, the echos were neat. U P was also there.

The thing went longer than we had thought, and we ended up missing our train to QLB. I hate that! We walked through the Fußgängerzone to kill time until the next train. I bought a key and change pouch thing and a small, small screwdriver set. They were only ,99DM apiece. After that we went to Extra to buy something so I could get change for the phone. We then bought our tickets at Bahnhof, and then called Sch. K and told her we would be a little late.

We got to K's only 15 minutes late from the original appointment time. I had a nice time, the mittags was also tasty. After we visited, we went by on B to make sure our appointment change was ok. We then went by on E and had a nice talk with him. He said he wants to start coming regularly to church again. Mal sehen.

After E we visited N. We also had a good talk with them. They gave u something to eat too.

We also visited the big castle on the hill after B. After n we came home. All in all, a good day.
