Mission Journal

Germany Hamburg, April 10, 1996 - April 4, 1998
Home Journal

I'm transcribing my mission journal on the day it happened 30 years later.

I wrote in my journal every day on my mission. Well, to be more accurate, if I missed a day, I wrote about it the next day. Every day was accounted for.

Why? I wanted to write a very basic Python static site generator for a while, and since I started my mission 30 years ago, I thought this was a perfect excuse to do that. So I made pysite.

I left out full names and sometimes use a different letter to represent someone's real name. Just in case. I also fixed most grammar issues. My spelling was atrocious. I left most of the mistakes in German grammar and spelling in place, because it's cute, like a little kid learning to talk. And I thought it would be cool to see the progression as I got better. Sometimes I'll add some present day commentary to the bottom of the post.

I cut my finger while cutting the cheese

October 15, 1996

Today went ok, best of all it went by quick. This morning we took the glass bottles to the recycling bin. Then we took Elder T's bike to Post and mailed that off. We met U P in the post office, and she told us that her dad was in the hospital. After the Post, we went to Kaufland and made copies of the TzT berichts. I got rid of 2DM worth of 10 pfennig stücken. After that we came home and had Mittags, and Elder N prepared for institute. I tried to make macaroni and cheese. One of these days I'll make it so it doesn't taste so floury.

After Mittags and everything we visited Brd. P in the hospital. He's not doing all that well, but I think he'll get better. We went right to Institute right after visiting with Brd P. There wasn't too many people at Institute today. Only Schw. B, Schw M, & Brd J. B came for the first half-hour, and J was there for the last 45 minutes or so. After Institute J took us with him to B's birthday party. That was a lot of fun. We sta around and talked, listened to music, ate food, and stuff like that.

After all that we came home. That was the fun for today.

Oh yeah, I got three letters today. One form Mom/Dad, and one from A. I also got one from K. The third thing was a small package with Halloween candy. Mom wrote "Missionary Supplies" instead of candy this time. I also cut my finger while cutting the cheese.
