Mission Journal

Germany Hamburg, April 10, 1996 - April 4, 1998
Home Journal

I'm transcribing my mission journal on the day it happened 30 years later.

I wrote in my journal every day on my mission. Well, to be more accurate, if I missed a day, I wrote about it the next day. Every day was accounted for.

Why? I wanted to write a very basic Python static site generator for a while, and since I started my mission 30 years ago, I thought this was a perfect excuse to do that. So I made pysite.

I left out full names and sometimes use a different letter to represent someone's real name. Just in case. I also fixed most grammar issues. My spelling was atrocious. I left most of the mistakes in German grammar and spelling in place, because it's cute, like a little kid learning to talk. And I thought it would be cool to see the progression as I got better. Sometimes I'll add some present day commentary to the bottom of the post.

I nearly had to pull him off the guy

October 01, 1996

Today went pretty well. We got our MSF checks cashed, and Elder N paid me back. Then we rushed to Bahnhof and missed tht train to QLB by 5 minutes. So then we went to Extra and bought some stuff to eat for Mittags while in QLB. I got fake Ritz crackers and a Kuchen. Then it was off to QLB. We did some TzT at first. We only did that for a little while. It was different. Some old, drunk guy forced us away from his building. Elder N got really ticked at the guy. I almost had to grab or pull him away. The guy was a real jerk. We took our lunch Pause after that, so Elder N could cool down. After that we went by on a couple of contacts and then rushed over to Frau S's. That went pretty well. We didn't make out a new Termin, though. After that we walked back to Bahnhof and came back to Halberstadt. Then we walked to Sch. B's for Institute. That went normal. B and I got in a little water fight at the end. We got a ride hom with J because it was raining pretty good.

The weather was pretty weird today. This morning it was 15˚C and sunny. By the end of Institute it was rainy and a lot cooler.

Well, that's all for today.
