Germany Hamburg, April 10, 1996 - April 4, 1998
Home Journal

I'm transcribing my mission journal on the day it happened 30 years later.

I wrote in my journal every day on my mission. Well, to be more accurate, if I missed a day, I wrote about it the next day. Every day was accounted for.

Why? I wanted to write a very basic Python static site generator for a while, and since I started my mission 30 years ago, I thought this was a perfect excuse to do that. So I made pysite.

I left out full names and sometimes use a different letter to represent someone's real name. Just in case. I also fixed most grammar issues. My spelling was atrocious. I left most of the mistakes in German grammar and spelling in place, because it's cute, like a little kid learning to talk. And I thought it would be cool to see the progression as I got better. Sometimes I'll add some present day commentary to the bottom of the post.

The days are flying by...

October 26, 1996

Another week forby. The days are flying by, some good, some bad. We all get some of both, no matter how many we've already had. Today, like many others, passed by with lightning speed. Wenn es mein Wille wurde, wähle ich ein andres Lied. Actually today was not too bad. This morning from 10:00 until this afternoon 14:00 we stayed inside. During this time Elder N prepared his talk for tomorrow. I tried to memorize the rest of Hauptgedanke 7. I accomplished only one paragraph. I should have finished the whole thing and been able to review the whole discussion. Unfortunately, I didn't.

We left at around 14:00 to visit Sch. B. We stopped by Sch. F and picked up something for Brd. P. Sch. F called Sch. B for us and found out that we couldn't visit her today. We left from Sch. F's too late and had to walk to Brd. P instead of taking the Bahn like planned.

We were only able to stay at P's for a few minutes, but that was still too long and we were 5 minutes late to Family K. We had one of our better visits today. We actually read in the BoM and answered some of her questions. We also talked about fasting with her and Herr K. We had Abendbrot with them and watched an old movie on TV that had a lot of naked ladies on it. I've had my fill of breasts! We only got home an half hour late, too.

Other than staying in for four hours, seeing a ton of naked ladies and coming home late, this day went pretty well. Maybe tomorrow will go better.
