Germany Hamburg, April 10, 1996 - April 4, 1998
Home Journal

I'm transcribing my mission journal on the day it happened 30 years later.

I wrote in my journal every day on my mission. Well, to be more accurate, if I missed a day, I wrote about it the next day. Every day was accounted for.

Why? I wanted to write a very basic Python static site generator for a while, and since I started my mission 30 years ago, I thought this was a perfect excuse to do that. So I made pysite.

I left out full names and sometimes use a different letter to represent someone's real name. Just in case. I also fixed most grammar issues. My spelling was atrocious. I left most of the mistakes in German grammar and spelling in place, because it's cute, like a little kid learning to talk. And I thought it would be cool to see the progression as I got better. Sometimes I'll add some present day commentary to the bottom of the post.

First Rouladen

September 01, 1996

Today was Fast Sunday, and it went pretty well. Church and everything went well. I almost got M K to bear his testimony. I was going to go up with him and help him, but we never got a chance. There was always someone who got up there faster than I could get M ready to go. Maybe next month. What made me really happy was that Frau K and P came for the last two hours of church. Hopefully it will be a forever thing.

After church we had lunch at J P's. We had "Rolladin" (Rouladen), my first time. It tasted good. After that we came back home and redid the zurückkehren list and I filled out a TzT bericht. Then we did some door to door on Becker Straße. We did that until it was time to come home and sing with Bruder J. We had a good sing, and then that was the end of the day pretty much.

There are still quite a few cookies left from when Elder C made the last Friday. He says he'll make them twice more before he leaves. I hope he does, because they taste good.

That's all for today's excitement. Bis Morgen.
