Mission Journal

Germany Hamburg, April 10, 1996 - April 4, 1998
Home Journal

I'm transcribing my mission journal on the day it happened 30 years later.

I wrote in my journal every day on my mission. Well, to be more accurate, if I missed a day, I wrote about it the next day. Every day was accounted for.

Why? I wanted to write a very basic Python static site generator for a while, and since I started my mission 30 years ago, I thought this was a perfect excuse to do that. So I made pysite.

I left out full names and sometimes use a different letter to represent someone's real name. Just in case. I also fixed most grammar issues. My spelling was atrocious. I left most of the mistakes in German grammar and spelling in place, because it's cute, like a little kid learning to talk. And I thought it would be cool to see the progression as I got better. Sometimes I'll add some present day commentary to the bottom of the post.

Today was a dud

October 07, 1996

Today was pretty much a dud. We left from Braunschweig about 9:00 and got to Halberstadt about 10:00. We came straight home and found out at about 10:40 that the Telecom had already come and didn't fix the phone. Then we helped out Frau F. We did the normal stuff, except we didn't have to buy anything. Also she showed us pictures. After that we came home and I took a shower. I really needed one. I hadn't had one since Saturday. I also had a nice shadow beard.

After that I filled out the rest of the contacts while Elder N prepared for Institute. After we did that for a while we went by on Herr K. We talked with him for a couple of minutes and then went to B's. We stayed there for a while and the bought Dönners on the way home. Tomorrow I find out if I'll be transferred or not. I don't really want to, but I know it'll happen soon. It's a weird feeling.
