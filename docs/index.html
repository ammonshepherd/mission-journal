Mission Journal

Germany Hamburg, April 10, 1996 - April 4, 1998
Home Journal

I'm transcribing my mission journal on the day it happened 30 years later.

I wrote in my journal every day on my mission. Well, to be more accurate, if I missed a day, I wrote about it the next day. Every day was accounted for.

Why? I wanted to write a very basic Python static site generator for a while, and since I started my mission 30 years ago, I thought this was a perfect excuse to do that. So I made pysite.

I left out full names and sometimes use a different letter to represent someone's real name. Just in case. I also fixed most grammar issues. My spelling was atrocious. I left most of the mistakes in German grammar and spelling in place, because it's cute, like a little kid learning to talk. And I thought it would be cool to see the progression as I got better. Sometimes I'll add some present day commentary to the bottom of the post.

Brown Mantle Man strikes again

September 20, 1996

Today went OK. I've had a cold for the past few days, and it's taking its toll on me. Today I slept in until 8:00am. It wasn't intentional. I guess my body needed the rest so it took it. Needless to say, I didn't get to much studying in. We left at about 10:00 to the Rathaus. There, Elder N and I "melded" ourselves "an". That took about an hour or two. Then we got Elder N's Monatskarte thing. After that we bought a few things at Kaufland so I could make cookies for Frau K. When we got home from that I made fake mashed potatoes with Würstchen. They actually tasted good, the potatoes and the Würstchen. After Mittags, I started on making the cookies. They all turned out well. I didn't even use what we bought at Kaufland, but at least we have it now. After the cookies were done, we headed over to K's. We saw Brown Mantle Man on the way there and had a good long talk with him, about 2 hours. Herr K saved us from going over 2 hours. He was driving through V-markt where we were talking with BMMan. He stopped and gave us a ride to his place, after we said goodbye to BMMan.

We visited with Frau and Herr K for a good hour and a half or so, then we came home.

Having a cold sucks! The weather has turned off pretty cold. I hate having a runny and stuffed up nose, and all the coughing.
