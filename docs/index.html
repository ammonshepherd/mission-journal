Mission Journal

Germany Hamburg, April 10, 1996 - April 4, 1998
Home Journal

I'm transcribing my mission journal on the day it happened 30 years later.

I wrote in my journal every day on my mission. Well, to be more accurate, if I missed a day, I wrote about it the next day. Every day was accounted for.

Why? I wanted to write a very basic Python static site generator for a while, and since I started my mission 30 years ago, I thought this was a perfect excuse to do that. So I made pysite.

I left out full names and sometimes use a different letter to represent someone's real name. Just in case. I also fixed most grammar issues. My spelling was atrocious. I left most of the mistakes in German grammar and spelling in place, because it's cute, like a little kid learning to talk. And I thought it would be cool to see the progression as I got better. Sometimes I'll add some present day commentary to the bottom of the post.

First blessing on the mission

September 08, 1996

Today went pretty good. Church went fine. Brd. J, Elder C and I sang well in Sacrament. It wasn't the best we've done, but it was OK. I felt the Spirit pretty good in Church, so it was good. After Church we had Mittags by D's. It was good eating. Brd. J was also invited to eat at D's. Brother D was sick today with bronchitis. He asked Elder C and me to give him a blessing. I felt real bad because I didn't know what I needed to say. Brd. D challenged me to give a blessing every month. I don't know where the opportunities will come from, but I'm going to take his challenge. I also need to memorize what I'm supposed to say in the anointing and blessing. It's not so much what I'm supposed to say, that I need work on, but actually doing it so that I can have practice and feel comfortable giving blessings.

After playing with M and T, then eating, and a blessing on Brd. D, Elder C and I went by on some zurückkehrens. We didn't have very much luck. Just an old lady that we had met before. She's really nice. When we go back next time we're going to try and get her interested in reading the Book of Mormon.

After kontakting we came home and sang with Brd J. That was all for this wonderful Sunday.
