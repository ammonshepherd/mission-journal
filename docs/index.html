Mission Journal

Germany Hamburg, April 10, 1996 - April 4, 1998
Home Journal

I'm transcribing my mission journal on the day it happened 30 years later.

I wrote in my journal every day on my mission. Well, to be more accurate, if I missed a day, I wrote about it the next day. Every day was accounted for.

Why? I wanted to write a very basic Python static site generator for a while, and since I started my mission 30 years ago, I thought this was a perfect excuse to do that. So I made pysite.

I left out full names and sometimes use a different letter to represent someone's real name. Just in case. I also fixed most grammar issues. My spelling was atrocious. I left most of the mistakes in German grammar and spelling in place, because it's cute, like a little kid learning to talk. And I thought it would be cool to see the progression as I got better. Sometimes I'll add some present day commentary to the bottom of the post.

Someone stole my candy!

August 28, 1996

Today was P-day so it was a pretty good day from the start. We had blueberry pancakes, they tasted very good. I also got an express delivery envelope that was supposed to have candy in it. But by the time it got to me someone had cut the envelope and stolen my candy. It made me mad to think someone would do that. It cost Mom & Dad $21 to send it and some JERK stole it all. That's just totally uncomprehensible. What kind of lowlife skum is he or she.

Well, after that bit of blood pumping anger was over with, the rest of the day went fine. It rained a whole bunch, so we didn't get to play B-Ball. I wrote a letter home, one to J, and one to K. After P-Day we had an appointment with Frau B. We wanted to show her videos, but the machine didn't work, so we read Joseph Smith's History. Hopefully she'll pray for understanding.

Right after that appointment we had Institute. Lucky for us that J came with us to the appointment so we could get a ride there and to Institute. That went well. Just the normal group today. We got a ride home from J, too. That's all for today.
