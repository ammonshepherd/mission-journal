Mission Journal

Germany Hamburg, April 10, 1996 - April 4, 1998
Home Journal

I'm transcribing my mission journal on the day it happened 30 years later.

I wrote in my journal every day on my mission. Well, to be more accurate, if I missed a day, I wrote about it the next day. Every day was accounted for.

Why? I wanted to write a very basic Python static site generator for a while, and since I started my mission 30 years ago, I thought this was a perfect excuse to do that. So I made pysite.

I left out full names and sometimes use a different letter to represent someone's real name. Just in case. I also fixed most grammar issues. My spelling was atrocious. I left most of the mistakes in German grammar and spelling in place, because it's cute, like a little kid learning to talk. And I thought it would be cool to see the progression as I got better. Sometimes I'll add some present day commentary to the bottom of the post.

Bumming around on P-day

September 25, 1996

Today was a pretty relaxed P-day. I pretty much reneged on my promise to myself to start studying better. This morning I didn't do hardly anything. (Nice English, huh!) I wrote a few letters, and pretty much bummed around. I also put the bike piece on that dad made for me. It works like a charm. Elder N and I also went to Post and looked in the Dom and the Martini Church. That was a little different because we did it in our P-day clothes. It was fun walking around in normal clothes. After that we came back and bummed around some more. I tried to make gravy stuff that goes over toast. I kind of messed it up at first, but then it ended up pretty good. The toast is another subject. I have a hard time making toast.

That's really everything that happened today!

I forgot, almost. We also had English class. There was 3 ladies and B. It went pretty well. We just talked, mostly in German. It was OK though.

B also brought me a guitar, so now I have to play for talent Abend. I practiced for an hour or so. I can remember how to play, I just can't remember the words to any of the songs. I tried or started to make up new words to one of them, but it's just not working. I'll think of something to do, I hope.
