Mission Journal

Germany Hamburg, April 10, 1996 - April 4, 1998
Home Journal

I'm transcribing my mission journal on the day it happened 30 years later.

I wrote in my journal every day on my mission. Well, to be more accurate, if I missed a day, I wrote about it the next day. Every day was accounted for.

Why? I wanted to write a very basic Python static site generator for a while, and since I started my mission 30 years ago, I thought this was a perfect excuse to do that. So I made pysite.

I left out full names and sometimes use a different letter to represent someone's real name. Just in case. I also fixed most grammar issues. My spelling was atrocious. I left most of the mistakes in German grammar and spelling in place, because it's cute, like a little kid learning to talk. And I thought it would be cool to see the progression as I got better. Sometimes I'll add some present day commentary to the bottom of the post.

The days do get better!

November 19, 1996

So the days do get better! Today was actually OK. We taught Frau W the first and gave her the Book of Mormon. It took two hours because she likes to talk. It was really sad. She's pregnant and smokes, drinks coffee, and all that bad stuff. She's killing her baby before it has a chance to live. It made me hurt for the kid. I wanted to tell her to quit smoking and stuff, but I didn't. Elder B wanted to do that too, so next time we will do it. We didn't get a fest appointment made, but we'll be back. She needs what the gospel can give her.

After that we came home and had a real quick lunch, then it was off to R's. She's doing well. We just answered some questions that she had while reading through Grundbegriffe des Evangeliums. She's really going to make it, and she'll be so strong. It's wonderful!

We then rushed to the Church to meet M and taught him the first. This guy was weird. He had some pretty deep questions. In the end he decided that this church isn't for him. So he went his merry way. We went to Familie V. They do our laundry. Brd. V and his daughter were the only ones there. We had a nice time, short, but nice Then we rushed to be on time for our appointment with Herr D. He wasn't there so we left a note. We did a little bit of TzT, then came home.
