Mission Journal

Germany Hamburg, April 10, 1996 - April 4, 1998
Home Journal

I'm transcribing my mission journal on the day it happened 30 years later.

I wrote in my journal every day on my mission. Well, to be more accurate, if I missed a day, I wrote about it the next day. Every day was accounted for.

Why? I wanted to write a very basic Python static site generator for a while, and since I started my mission 30 years ago, I thought this was a perfect excuse to do that. So I made pysite.

I left out full names and sometimes use a different letter to represent someone's real name. Just in case. I also fixed most grammar issues. My spelling was atrocious. I left most of the mistakes in German grammar and spelling in place, because it's cute, like a little kid learning to talk. And I thought it would be cool to see the progression as I got better. Sometimes I'll add some present day commentary to the bottom of the post.

3 hour chat with a theologian

November 22, 1996

Today was pretty weird. This morning we made R's stop smoking pills. We just changed the covers on Mentos. We did that and also Elder B translated something for Sch. F until we had to leave for Sch. H's. We were fed really well there. I was to the point of bursting at the end. It tasted wonderful.

After that we had planned to do TzT for 4 hours. We only got an. half hour because 3 hours was spent talking to this guy who is studying theology. He's going to be a Father. Anyhow, we got into some deep, deep stuff. It was crazy. He was real nice and wasn't trying to tear us apart or anything. He was just comparing our truths with his beliefs. Of course he thought his beliefs were the truth, but... Anyhow, it was pretty draining. We tried the first discussion, but we kept delving into the deep realms of stuff.

After that we went to K's, but only the kids were there. The problem with them, I think, is that they don't understand how important everything really is, so they aren't making an effort to make progress. It's sad.

This whole world is pretty darn messed up.
