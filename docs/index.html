Mission Journal

Germany Hamburg, April 10, 1996 - April 4, 1998
Home Journal

I'm transcribing my mission journal on the day it happened 30 years later.

I wrote in my journal every day on my mission. Well, to be more accurate, if I missed a day, I wrote about it the next day. Every day was accounted for.

Why? I wanted to write a very basic Python static site generator for a while, and since I started my mission 30 years ago, I thought this was a perfect excuse to do that. So I made pysite.

I left out full names and sometimes use a different letter to represent someone's real name. Just in case. I also fixed most grammar issues. My spelling was atrocious. I left most of the mistakes in German grammar and spelling in place, because it's cute, like a little kid learning to talk. And I thought it would be cool to see the progression as I got better. Sometimes I'll add some present day commentary to the bottom of the post.

Cookies, spaghetti and cheese crisps

August 30, 1996

Today was a good day. Elder C made cookies and we had Distrikt Versammlung at the same time. We had Mittags right after all that. I made Aldi's spaghetti for my first time. After that we dropped a few things by Schwester B, and then went to our appointment with H. Unfortunately, he wasn't there, so we did some TzT on Leo-Tolstoi Straße. That took a while. Then we went to visit Schwester M, but she wasn't home either. We saw A on the street and we talked with her for a minute, then we were off to Post where we mailed Elder H his tie and Elders H & M an Empfählung. Then we came home and got the cookies to take to D's. We had them on their plate and gave them cookies to say thanks for the potato salad. We ended up eating a few cheese crisps. I also got the keys to the old U-lock.

Then we visited with Frau K for a while. We gave them some cookies too.

That was all the fun for today.
