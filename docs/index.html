Mission Journal

Germany Hamburg, April 10, 1996 - April 4, 1998
Home Journal

I'm transcribing my mission journal on the day it happened 30 years later.

I wrote in my journal every day on my mission. Well, to be more accurate, if I missed a day, I wrote about it the next day. Every day was accounted for.

Why? I wanted to write a very basic Python static site generator for a while, and since I started my mission 30 years ago, I thought this was a perfect excuse to do that. So I made pysite.

I left out full names and sometimes use a different letter to represent someone's real name. Just in case. I also fixed most grammar issues. My spelling was atrocious. I left most of the mistakes in German grammar and spelling in place, because it's cute, like a little kid learning to talk. And I thought it would be cool to see the progression as I got better. Sometimes I'll add some present day commentary to the bottom of the post.

11 hours with our 'gators

November 03, 1996

Today was definitely NOT a normal Sunday. First of all, it was Pfahl Konferenz (Stake Conference) in Hannover. Second Familie K went and we drove up with them. That wa sa two hour drive up. Nothing too exciting happened on the way up, ora t Pfahl Conference. I talked with M. He is really messed up. He's set his mind up that nobody wants to be his friend, and that I'm his only chance at having a friend. I kept on telling him he needs to try harder to make other friends. We'll see.

The way home from Conference was WAY not normal. We drove through the Harz on the way back. We also stopped in a few places along the way. One of which was the McDonalds in Goslar. Not really a Sunday activity, but! That was my first McDonald's in over a year. It was really good, even though it was Sunday. After that we stopped at a place with a man-made waterfall, and there was also a restaurant over the stream. It was a pretty cool place. Something like the smallest kingdom in Germany is what it was. After that, we stopped by another dam. It was actually pretty boring, but oh well. All in all, it was a good trip. We started the day with them at 7:15am, and got back home ending the time at 18:00. A total of about 11 hours at one time with our investigators. Wie gesagt, it was fun except for that we reek like cigarettes.

Also today, we had Abendbrot and read in the Book of Mormon with Brd. J, here at our place. That went by pretty good. What was really cool on the way up, we passed through where the Wall (separating East and West Germany) used to be. You could really tell it was there. Everything in the area was still pretty dead and still. I thought it was cool.
