Mission Journal

Germany Hamburg, April 10, 1996 - April 4, 1998
Home Journal

I'm transcribing my mission journal on the day it happened 30 years later.

I wrote in my journal every day on my mission. Well, to be more accurate, if I missed a day, I wrote about it the next day. Every day was accounted for.

Why? I wanted to write a very basic Python static site generator for a while, and since I started my mission 30 years ago, I thought this was a perfect excuse to do that. So I made pysite.

I left out full names and sometimes use a different letter to represent someone's real name. Just in case. I also fixed most grammar issues. My spelling was atrocious. I left most of the mistakes in German grammar and spelling in place, because it's cute, like a little kid learning to talk. And I thought it would be cool to see the progression as I got better. Sometimes I'll add some present day commentary to the bottom of the post.

Latest Post

I REALLY, REALLY hate being late.

October 29, 1996

Today started out not so good, as a matter of fact I was downright MAD. We missed our Straßenbahn and because of that our train to QLB. We missed the Bahn because someone wasn't ready on time. We had to take the train an hour later and because of that we didn't ge to meet with Herr B. We stopped by and said sorry and made a new appointment. Herr B didn't seem too happy, I wasn't either. We then went by on a few contacts, none of which were home. A lady stopped us and wants us to come talk to an English class she goes to. We gave her our number, and she'll call. That was one good thing about today, not the lady, but this morning our phone RANG. THAT'S RIGHT OUR PHONE WORKS!! It's about time.

We came home from our pretty fruitless trip to QLB and had Mittags. I made a tomato soup and put in corn and Rindfleisch (ground beef). Then it was a corn soup. I can hardly believed I made it, let alone ate it. I'm progressing.

After Mittags, and a little nap for me, I finished reading the September Ensign while Elder N prepared for Institute. That went pretty well. After it, we had our first meeting with J as our new GML. Then we had a little bit of Abendbrot with Brd. J. And that's all.

Today went well. I was really mad because we missed the train and all that stuff. I REALLY, REALLY hate being late for everything. I also hate not being able to follow the rules because my comp. won't. We'll see how it goes if I get made a senior one of these days. The end of today turned out a lot better than the beginning.
