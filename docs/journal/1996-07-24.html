100DM on groceries

Wednesday, Jul 24, 1996
Location: Halberstadt

It's always great to have a P-Day. Unless you've got to spend about 100DM for groceries. Granted we shop for two weeks worth, but 100DM is a little much. I'm out of all my MSF now. I was actually out two weeks ago. This month was just terrible in regards to money.

Anyhow, today went pretty well. I got a lot of cleaning done since Pres. B is coming on Friday. I guess the highlight, or rather the most important part of the day, was our Termin (appointmnet) with Frau P. We had Brüder J with us as a joint teach. It went alright. We had to read over the chapter together because she couldn't remember what she read. That took a while, so we only got through the first Hauptgedanke. And our next appointment isn't until two weeks. I sure hope she reads and prays and remembers.
