Mission Journal

Germany Hamburg, April 10, 1996 - April 4, 1998
HOME JOURNAL

God answers prayers!

Saturday, Sep 07, 1996
Location: Halberstadt

Today was a weird day. This morning we walked through the Altstadt Fest a couple of times. It was a weird feeling while we were there, for me. I only tried to talk with a few people, under 6, instead of all of the people. I just didn't have a peaceful feeling there like I do usually. The Spirit was just not there, and I guess I was scared to talk with people. We came home after that. On the way home I was pondering the feelings I was having and pleading with Heavenly Father to help us with the work and to help me. Then something cool happened. A guy in a car pulled up beside us and asked if we were Americans. He was holding up traffic so he went around the corner and parked, so we we went to talk with him. He was a big American fan, and had had earlier contact with missionaries in Hannover, where he lives. He said that he was really interested in the Church, and he is in Wernigerode for today and tomorrow, so he'll come here to Church tomorrow. We also gave him a Book of Mormon. Hopefully he will come, and Familie K also.

When we got home we ate Mittags and then Elder C finished his box and did some packing for about four hours. Then we went to Sch. M for Abendbrot. After that we came back and sang with Brd. J, we practiced for tomorrow. We also sung "I know that my Redeemer lives" and "I need thee every hour." They brought the Spirit in strong, it was awesome!

The day started sour and turned out sweet. Hopefully tomorrow will go well. God answers prayers!
