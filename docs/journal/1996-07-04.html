First 4th! Or 80 Old Ladies in a row

Thursday, Jul 04, 1996
Location: Halberstadt

Today is the Fourth of July. We had a Distriktsversammlung with American songs and theme. I made fry bread and we also had watermelon and cool-aid. B ate with us, too, and she brought sparklers. There was about 40 and we used them all. It was cool. After all that we did some Tür zu Tür. Most of it was in an old folks apartment complex. It was a huge apartment building for old people. Whew! That's all I can say.

After that we tried to see if we could have our appointment with the couple we met on Am Sommerbad today instead of tomorrow, Friday. They had company, so we'll do that next week sometime. Then we did more Tür zu Tür. We talked with a man who really made me mad, because he kept saying there was no God, and that our church is just like all the others. They guys at the top get paid a bunch of money. What made me mad was that he was going on and talking about stuff that he thought he knew about, but actually didn't. That just about wiped us out, so after another couple of places we came home.

The thing that sticks out most about today is the letter I got from D. It's real nice and all, especially if what she says is true. I'm just real doubtful. I hope to high heavens that she's being sincere. I've been in something, maybe love or what not, with her for a while. That's why I'm doubtful. Because of the way she is and because it's been a dream that suddenly comes true. I just don't know what to think. When I write her next Wednesday, I'll ask her to tell the truth!

I originally wrote this as July 5, 1996, but then realized I was writing about July 4!

This day was a tuff day. We started out the day doing Tür zu Tür. Like normal, nobody was interested. At the end of the street we were doing was a big apartment building. We thought great, lots of chances here. What a surprise. Behind every door in that building was an old lady. Nothing against old ladies, but 80 of them in a row was enough for me. Then we tried another street, with not much luck there. We talked with a guy for an hour, and he told us how our church is like all the others. He said that all our tything goes to the guys at the top, and a bunch of stuff like that. It made me mad because he has no idea what he's talking about. He was just assuming we were like all the other churches. Well, we're not! Because our church was organized by Christ and is run by Christ, and not by man.

Anyhow, I was real tired and what not after this day.

Well, I feel real stupid! All this happened on Thursday not Friday.
