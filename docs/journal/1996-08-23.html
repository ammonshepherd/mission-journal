Visiting lots of Zurückkehrens

Friday, Aug 23, 1996
Location: Halberstadt

Today was a busy day. We visited a lot of Zurückkehrens the whole day. We actually only visited a few, but tried to reach many. We fianlly got a hold of Frau P. We read a little bit in the Book of Mormon, and also invited her and her brother to "Spiel des Lebens" on Tuesday. She seemed real interested to come to that. Hopefully she will. After that we came home and ate lunch. Then we visited Herr L and dropped a note by Familie K. Then we did some TzT on those that weren't at home before. After that we did some more Zurückkehrens and actually got let in on one. She was a lady that had had earlier contact with the church as a kid. We talked with her for a while and gave her a Book of Mormon. She seemed real enthused to talk with us about her feelings about the Church and everything. Hopefully she'll get even more interested and something great will happen.

Today in the Fußgängerzone, I saw a map of the Halberstadt area. It was a neat cartoon type map. It costs 24DM, but I'm going to buy it anyhow, I think. It's really awesome.

That's all for today. Nothing too exciting happened. That's probably for the better. Na ja!
