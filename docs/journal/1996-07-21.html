A real first discussion!

Sunday, Jul 21, 1996
Location: Halberstadt

Today went pretty well. church went all normal. Schwester Z brought us our Mittags since we aren't allowed to eat at her place. She's the one that plays the organ. It tasted alright. She gave us Würste, peas and dessert. So we mixed the peas, Würste with rice. The dessert was good auch (also).

After Mittags, we finished up the TzT Berichts that we had, and I copied the names and addresses of people we can visit this week from the ward list to my planner.

Then we did a bit of TzT, without any luck, until our appointment with Frau P.

Wow! That went awesome. We read Alma 32 after we answered a few questions that she had. And best of all she still has interest! We even made an appointment to teach or rather discuss the 2nd Lektion. Yeah, yeah-hoo, yippee, hoot-hoot-hoot. Ich bin sehr begeistert.

Those are pretty much the highlights for today. Also I spoke with Br. D today about a bike. He has one that he bought from a missionary. I'll take a look at it next week when we have Mittags there. Hopefully, it'll be in a good condition and Br. D will want to sell it cheap.
