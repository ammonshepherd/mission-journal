Mission Journal

Germany Hamburg, April 10, 1996 - April 4, 1998
HOME JOURNAL

Real pizza!

Wednesday, Oct 09, 1996
Location: Halberstadt

Today was pretty cool. We went Einkaufen with Bruder P in the morning. After that we hung around the Wohnung. I wrote a letter home and one to R and one to C. Mainly because I got one from both of them today. I was lazy and didn't write anybody else. I also cleaned my little dresser thing by my bed. It's really clean now. I also made a cake with a German cake mix. It turned out pretty well.

After a long period of time passed with just a few things occurring, we went weight lifting. It was a lot better today. I worked myself good, but not too much. After that we had English class. That went pretty well. I mostly helped B write a response to her au-pair-Mädchen thing.

After that B invited us to have pizza with her and her brother. We ate real pizza in the church. We mostly talked about cuss words. Elder N was asking B's brother what a lot of words he had heard were, and how bad they are.

After they were gone, we came up. I played with my yo-yo. The string on the yellow one broke twice and I jerry-rigged it so now it works. I also figured out how to do the star. I practiced it until I could do it well. Now I'm going to work on the Eifel tower. I might ask home for a couple of yo-yo strings or even a new and better yo-yo. Mal sehen.
