HOME JOURNAL

Sore feet

Saturday, Aug 24, 1996
Location: Halberstadt

My feet hurt sooo bad at the end of the today! We tried a few contacts this morning until it was time to meet with Bruder J. On the way back we walked through the park, my first time. Luckily, it was cool, so we had no unwanted naked ladies walking around. I hope I never see that problem! Anyhow, on the way home, Elder C also got a box for his bike, a real bike box. Then we bought some cakes for Mittags. We had lunch with Bruder J in the Keller today. He didn't have much time so he just ate and ran. That was OK. After Mittags, and after Elder C took his daily nach Mittags poop, we headed out to try and reach more contacts. We reached a few more, most, if not all, had no interest. Then we did some TzT on Nordweg and Sudweg streets. We actually talked with a few people, and one or two or more, but not many, said to come back. Only one of those seemed really interested to talk with us. After that we came home.

It rained a little bit here and there today. Nothing big. I did the bericht as we did TzT. I was happy today and was not annoyed at all by my friend and comp, Elder C. That made me happy. Just for info and space filler, I'm ready Die Wahrheit Wiederhergestelt, in German of course. I understand most of it, and find it very interesting. I only read a page or so during study time or lunch so it goes slow.
