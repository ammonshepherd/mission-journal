Mission Journal

Germany Hamburg, April 10, 1996 - April 4, 1998
HOME JOURNAL

Ping pong for 2 hours

Wednesday, Jul 17, 1996
Location: Halberstadt

This day went pretty well. Mostly because it was P-Day. Ever since Elder C got transferred my studying has gone down the drain. Today was no exception. I need to work on that better.

Elder C and I cleaned the apartment right after study time. After a little bit of resting, we went to get Elder C his plastic thing for his Monatskarte. We also visited the Dom and checked a few stores for small hats for Elder C's little brother.

Then we came home and read letters and I wrote a few. The one home wsa four pages. Then we set up the ping-pong table really well. So now the net is tight and stays put, and we extended the table. We played for two hours.

Then at 18:00 we dropped a note that Elder C had written for the D's by their place. Sister D said they were just going to be eating in a few minutes so we felt obligated to stay and eat. We also got to know them better. I played with M, too, he's a cool little kid. He can speak German and English! He'll only speak English with the missionaries because he knows we can. So it all worked out well, we both got to know the D's a little better and we got fed.

By the time that was done, it was too late to do door to door or anything, so we came home. We played table-tennis with the other Elders for a while and that was it. The whole day.
