Zone Conference

Wednesday, Aug 21, 1996
Location: Halberstadt

Today was Zone Conference day. We awoke at the good early hour of 4:30am and we were out the door by 5:00. We rode the train forever and arrived in Hannover at about 9:00. We had plenty of time to get to the Church and we did so without any problem. We met up with the Kassel Elders at the Bahnhof again. This Zone Conference was really cool because all of the Elders from my MTC district were there except for Elder R and Former Elder G. So I got to see Elder M again. It was cool. I can understand him now, and speak a lot more Deutsch back.

The thing I liked most about this Zone Conference was the Spirit that was there. It was just totally strong and awesome. The Area representative for our area spoke to us, along with his wife, Pres. and Sis. B, the normal three talks and the four finishers, one of which was Elder C. There was also a few musical numbers. The Spirit was just so strong there. It was the best Zone Conference yet. I didn't take too many notes, because I was just basking in the love of the Lord and the Spirit.

After that, on the way home, we stopped at Wernigerode and met a lady with whom we had had an appointment with. We also did a few TzT Zurückkehrens. Then we came home. We had a long day.

We also brought lunch to eat on the way home. It was a long tiring day!
