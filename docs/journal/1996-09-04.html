The bike story

Wednesday, Sep 04, 1996
Location: Halberstadt

Well, there is only one week left for Elder C. Today was is big bike day. Where he had planned to take his bike apart. How did it go? You'll have to wait to hear the rest of this story.

First thing, this morning I did 135 pushups and 150 crunches. I think today was my best work out yet. My arms were burning! We went shopping today. We went to Aldi with Bruder P, and then went to the Post to send packages. I sent everything I wanted to except my BoM, and then we went to Kaufland. All in all we only spent 29.81DM or something. I also bought a socket wrench set with P.G. (personal Geld) Elder C needed it to take his bike apart, and I wanted one, so I bought it. Shopping took from 9:00 until 12:00, that's with an hour at the post.

After shopping, Elder C started on his bike. I wrote a letter home, one to T S and another one to K. I'm getting messed up by K. I really like her, and I don't think she likes me as much as she makes it sound like. I'm going to be hurting when I get "Dear John"ed. Oh well!

Elder C's bike story: He worked hard and all day. It was A LOT harder than he thought. He had trouble with lots of things. Now it's going a little better, he just needs to make the box now.

After P-Day time, actually an hour earlier, we visited Sch. F and the Brd. J. That was about it for missionary work. We did give a BoM to B.

I've been trying to write neat and stuff, but it takes too much time so I screwed it. Life's going good. I get a new comp in a week. Being a missionary is awesome! I got a letter from R T and J and C.
