Epic first bike ride in Germany

Thursday, Aug 08, 1996
Location: Halberstadt

Well, we did it! We rode to Quedlinburg with bikes!!

First off, this morning we said good bye to Elders H and A. It was great getting to know them, they're great guys.

After that, it was off on my first Fahrrad tour. The ride there gave me problems for some reason. I just had a hard time. No energy or something. Our Termin with Herr B fell out because he is in the hospital. So we visited Brd. N. I know I'm blessed because after riding to QLB, I had a massive hunger. We had arrived at N's around lunch time, so they gave us some soup.

Then we visited Familie K to get to know them better, and we were blessed again. (More food I mean.) I felt real bad, because Schwester K didn't really look too happy to see us. She made us eat, though. I met their older son, though, that I didn't know about.

After Familie K, we tried a few Kontakts, but none were home. Then we had an appointment with E. N. We ended up being 20 minutes late because we forgot the address. So at first we looked around for 15 minutes. We look at all the buildings on the street, but we had passed over it on our search through all the buildings. We had a nice little chat with E, then it was time to ride back to Halberstadt. Don't think that's all, the fun is only beginning.

Wow! That was the most fun I"ve had so far in almost my whole life. Instead of taking the Bundesstraße back to Halberstadt, we decided to take some back roads so that there wouldn't be so much traffic to ride with. There wasn't anybody with us so that part went as planned. The roads weren't like we expected, and there were a few more than there was on the map. The good thing was we didn't get lost. The bad thing (but probably a good thing) was that we didn't get to take the back roads all the way to Halberstadt, because the road to get there was turned into a farm field.

Let me start with the highlights and from the beginning. Everything from the start went fine. I was having a blast because it was my first time doing real mountain biking. The first fork in the road we came to wasn't on the map, but since one was overgrown, we took the other. At the fork we went up a hill and came down a lot farther than we went up. It was awesome. I was hauling down a dirt road that was in bad shape. It was AWESOME. We then came to another fork, actually a place with tractors and stuff. I think they are for clearing the road because there were no fields in the area, but who can say? Anyhow, our only choice was to go forwards. That meant going up the nill in a dry river bed, or a very, very bad road. For me that was awesome! Then we came to another fork in the road, a 4-way split! One went to the Bundesstraße, one went somewhere, one went back the way we came, and the other was supposed to go to Halberstadt. We took that, and it was an even worse road than all the others put together.

For the most part, there were two big ruts, some places 1 1/2 feet deep, on either side of a mound that served as a road. To make it worse, the ruts were muddy and sometimes filled with water. So for the most part, I tried to ride on the mound. It was tricky and potentially dangerous in parts. My bike did slide out from me a couple of times, but I was able to catch myself before I fell.

Anyhow, we took that road until it turned into a field full of crops. So then we turned around and was forced to take the Bundesstraße. The little road there was cool. Almost to the end there was a stretch of about ten yards or more of just mud. I just drove through, slowly of course. Elder C was always behind. As he came out he was walking. I thought at first he just couldn't ride through, but his tire was FLAT! We crossed the street where there was a big puddle of water and there we fixed the first flat tire I've ever had to on the road, in my whole life!! Also my first on my mission and in Germany.

Anyhow, we got that fixed real well and started off to Halberstadt on the Bundesstraße. For some reason I was full of energy on the way home and I was cookin'! I had it on the highest gear. The biggest in the front and the smallest in the back, and I was pumpin' fast! It was AWESOME! I loved it!

The only thing schlecht is that my seat is hard as a rock, so my po hurts like the dickens!

Now we are in the empty apartment. Taking stuff, calling people, seeing what kind of things need to be cleaned, and relaxing.
