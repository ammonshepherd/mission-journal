HOME JOURNAL

My first phone call in Deutschland

Monday, Jul 22, 1996
Location: Halberstadt

We visited Schwester B this morning. That all went OK. A bit of back information. I was the one that called her last night and set up the appointment and stuff, all by myself. My first phone call in Deutschland! Wow!

After that, we did service for the other Elders by doing their normal service. We took some garbage out for an old lady and also bought her a bunch of fish. Then we talked with her for a while. That would have been all fine and dandy, but her house REEKED. I've never smelt anything so bad. Yuck!

After that we came home and I made macaroni and cheese. It wasn't as good as last time, but it's something to eat. After that, at about 14:00 we did TzT. We did that until 18:30. Needless to say, my feet were DEAD. After that we came home and sang with Bruder J. We also made Super Berichts. Now we have Berichts with the lines filling up the whole page, front and back. They're awesome.

The work here is going fine. It feels like there's something to do now that we are copying all the Berichts over and are starting to go through all the old streets again in hopes of contacting everyone.

I'm also worried about money. I've spent a whole lot already. I have only 30DM for Einkaufen (shopping) for Wednesday. There's a lot of money I spent somewhere, and I don't know where it went.
