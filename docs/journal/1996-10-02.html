Mission Journal

Germany Hamburg, April 10, 1996 - April 4, 1998
HOME JOURNAL

o.k. to dumb

Wednesday, Oct 02, 1996
Location: Halberstadt

Today went from o.k. to dumb. I was really lazy this morning. I decided to sleep in for a long time. I finally got up when I thought it was around 9:00. To my surprise, it was only 7:00. I then took a shower for lack of anything else to do. After that I made pancakes. Then after I ate, I read a chapter in the BoM and then finished letters. I was lazy again and only wrote home and to K because I had pictures to send to her. Then after I hung around and waited for Elder N to get ready and finish eating, we went to get our hair cuts. After that Elder N bought a birthday card and then we went to Post. I sent my pictures and a few week planners home. I sent by Luftpost because it had my letter with it. It cost 16DM. Then we came home and waited for B to pick us up and take us with her to the gym. That was pretty fun. I worked out pretty light. At the end I rode the exercise bike for 10 minutes. I got 7.4Km. Afterwards I got real light headed and almost puked. I went to the bathroom and dry heaved. After that we came home and waited for English class. That went pretty well. B bought us Überraschungsei. I got an awesome toy. B's car wouldn't start when she tried to go home, so we tried to help. It was flooded and that was all I could tell. There was no carburetor, so I had no idea what to do. We waited around for an hour, then gave up when it wouldn't start and numerous tries. B was really, really mad. It was a cruddy ending to an o.k. day.
