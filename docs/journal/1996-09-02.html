HOME JOURNAL

It's getting chilly

Monday, Sep 02, 1996
Location: Halberstadt

Everything went well for me today. This morning we met with Herr R. He had remembered that today would be the last time for him to see Elder C, so he had a few cookies and a little something to drink. I thought that was pretty cool. After that we helped Frau F. Then we took our Mittags Pause, and Elder C prepared for Institute. Then we had an appointment with a Herr W, but he ended up not being there. It was weird because his name was on the klingel, but where his apartment should have been looked empty, and there was no mail box for him either. So he probably gave us a false or old address. Instead of the appointment we finished doing door to door on Becker Str., and then tried some zurückkehrens in Sargstedter Weg area. After all that fun we left a note by Familie K, and then did some more Kontakts on Rudolf-Breitscheid and Minna-Bollmann Straßes. Then it was dark so we came home.

It started to get chilly in the mornings and evenings. This morning it was 9˚C and this evening it is 8˚ almost 7˚C. I'm glad that it's cool enough that I don't melt during the day. Hopefully it won't get all that cold either. Only time will tell.

That's all for now.
