Mission Journal

Germany Hamburg, April 10, 1996 - April 4, 1998
HOME JOURNAL

I gave a Book of Mormon to a nun

Tuesday, Oct 08, 1996
Location: Halberstadt

Today went pretty O.K. I haven't been transferred and I'm happy about that. I actually like it here. Everybody else thinks it's the scum city of the mission, but it's home to me.

Anyhow, this morning we worked in Quedlinburg. We had our appointment with Herr B. His back was hurting him and he wasn't doing too well health wise. After visiting B we tried a couple of contacts. None of which were home. Then we went and ate lunch in the park. After that we did some more door to door on Kate-Kollwitz Straße. It was a repeat of last time. Not so bad though. We did have a good talk with an old guy whose wife had Alzheimers disease. He started crying while talking to us. I felt really sorry for him.

After that we came home because Elder N was getting really mad again. At home Elder N prepared for Institute and I read my three letters from home. I also got pictures from B & H's wedding.

Institute went OK. B was still mad at us. Oh well! We then went with J to find out about working in the Katholische Suppe kitchen or whatever it's called. We talked with two nuns and a priest. We also talked with them about the Church (ours). We also gave them a Book of Mormon. One of the nuns said she would read the Lesevorschläge (reading suggestions).

After that we came home and waited around to see if I would get transferred. No one called, so Elder N called Elder L to find out. And I'm staying.

That's all folks!
