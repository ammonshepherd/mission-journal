Germany Hamburg, April 10, 1996 - April 4, 1998
HOME JOURNAL

Another drunk story

Tuesday, Sep 10, 1996
Location: Halberstadt

I need to write something that happened yesterday before I forget it. Before we went to Fam. K, or on our way there, we stopped and talked to the Halberstädter drunks. They are two guys that I've seen around a lot, and they are always drunk as a skunk. We talked with them a little bit, and one gave us a bottle of apple juice. As the other one shook our hands to say goodbye he tipped over and fell flat on the ground. We picked him up and sent them on their way, and told them to quit drinking alcohol.

We poured the apple juice in the toilet because it smelled like beer. Na ja, that's my second drunk story.

Today went OK. First off this morning we took care of all of Elder C's things by the Landratsamt, and got him Abmelded. Then we came home for Mittags. Elder C also prepared for Institute. Then we took the D's scale back, and also took some bottles in for Pfand, for money. We both bought Überraschungs eir. I got another cheap prize. After that we went by on a few contacts, and also had a little talk with Herr K. Then it was off to Institute. That went well. I translated for A again, and did a little better this time. We brought cookies and A loves them. After Institute, we had Abendbrot by P's. We hadn't know that, but it was cool, so we didn't get Döners because of that. Oh well!

From the weekly letter home:

Tuesday was weird. We did a lot of things to get Elder C ready to leave. Then we did some other missionary stuff until Institute. We brought some home made chocolate chip cookies I made with us. Ashley, the exchange student from Idaho, loved them. It was the first time she had American cookies since she got here. After Institute, we had Abendbrot at P's. After that we came home and waited for my new comp. to call. We waited fro two hours and he never called so we went to bed.
