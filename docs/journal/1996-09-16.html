HOME JOURNAL

Going to Braunscwheig

Monday, Sep 16, 1996
Location: Halberstadt 
Hallo Familie!

This week was a doosy! A whole lot has happened. The highlights from the past are big ones. An explanation really quick. the reason my writing is all messy is because I'm writing this on a train. The first highlight was getting a new comp. You already knew that from my last letter. But I've been with him for a week now, so I know him a little better. He's still cool! I think we'll get along really well. Anyhow, yesterday, Mon. 16, in the evening we left for Braunschweig. Because this is counting as my journal for the Mon, Tues, Wed, I'll be a little more explicit (I'll give more details!) So, on Mon we met with a lady with whom I hope we can start the discussions with. After that we went to the old lady that we help out with every Monday morning. This time was a little different because her hair manacure person was coming in a little while after we got there. So all that we did was take out her garbage and then left. After that we went home and Elder N packed for our trip to Braunschweig. Then we had a nice talk with our only real investigator. Unfortunately, it was only with the wife, but we read a scripture with her and I think she felt the Spirit. I hope so. We were there for 2 hours talking. We then had to rush home, grab our bags, and run out the door to catch the Straßenbahn to the Hauptbahnhof (train depot). Then it took 3 hours to get to Braunschweig. Now you may be wondering why I'm going to Braunschweig.
