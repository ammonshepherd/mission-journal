Messed up the fried rice

Tuesday, Jul 23, 1996
Location: Halberstadt

Today was just fine and dandy. We did some more TzT this morning, until lunch. I tried to make fried rice for lunch. I screwed it up real bad. Luckily I was able to fix it up so it was eatable. After lunch I took a nap while Elder C prepared for Institute. After a while, we headed back out to do some more TzT before Institute. And by George, that's exactly what we did. Institute went all fine and dandy, too. It was at Schwester M's so we could watch a video. Immer noch speise und Getränk, so ich war Glücklich. (Still food and drink so I was happy.) After Institute, though, we stayed and looked through all her pictures again. After that we just came home. My feet are terribly sore, and it was definitely summer today. Other than the head and tiredness and Elder C's dumb jokes, the day went by fine.

I'm really, really tired. I'm going to bed.
