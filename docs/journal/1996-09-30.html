Mission Journal

Germany Hamburg, April 10, 1996 - April 4, 1998
HOME JOURNAL

I even liked the Rotkohl

Monday, Sep 30, 1996
Location: Halberstadt

Today went pretty well. We had a cool meeting with Frau P. We talked about the Auferstehung, resurrection, and about God hearing prayers. I hope she will pray and believe. After we visited Frau P we did our service at Frau F. As we bought potatoes at V-markt I bought a Kinder Eir. It was a cheesy prize.

Right after F's we went to P's for Mittags. We got nice and stuffed there. It was cool, because I like everything, even the Rotkohl. After that we came home so Elder N could prepare for Institute. I was going to do something, but I ended up sleeping for the hour and a half instead. Then we went to K's for Home Teaching. We ended up getting Abendbrot, too. We also got about 50 apples. We were there for about 2 hours. It was pretty fun. I got to know them a little better.

After that we came home. I started a new contact list. It has more than doubled in size, because of all the new ones we found. Most of them will probably say Kein Interesse, but oh well.
