Eating raw hamburger

Tuesday, Sep 24, 1996
Location: Halberstadt

Today went OK. This morning was really cruddy. I was so tired that I pretty much just slept until we had to leave. We worked in Quedlinburg today. We almost didn't catch the train because the lines to buy tickets were long and slow. I got a ticket, but Elder N didn't. We ended up going to the train anyhow, and Elder N was going to buy one on the train. I say "he was going to", because he never did. The conductor never came by to stamp our tickets, so Elder N got a free ride there.

We visited Herr B first thing. We had a nice talk with him. We also had raw hamburger. It was pretty sick, but what could we do! After Herr B we went by on Frau S. She was kind of mad that we ditched her last week, but there was nothing we could do. She wasn't too mad because we made a new appointment. AFter that we had Mittags by Fam. K. I gave a spiritual thought, and I felt it went pretty well. After that, we came home and Elder N prepared for Institute. Then we went there, had it, and came back.

There was a commercial or something being made in Quedlinburg while we were there. We saw them in the Fußgängerzone and also at the Bahnhof.

That's about all from today.
