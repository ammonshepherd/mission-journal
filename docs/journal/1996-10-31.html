First Halloween in Germany

Thursday, Oct 31, 1996
Location: Halberstadt

HAPPY HALLOWEEN! Today was pretty OK for me. I made pancakes in Braunschweig. We had our door to door/Trick or Treat deal. It was pretty awesome. I went with Elder S. We had a lot of fun. We really went Trick or Treating too. We would say "Trick or Treat" as soon as they opened the door. Then we would lead that into introducing ourselves and the Church. It was cool and worked a couple of times. The people were so astonished that they couldn't say "Kein Interesse", so we were able to talk with them for a while. We actually got let in once. It was a really old lady who was a little senile, but we talked with her for a while about prayer and stuff. Nothing else too exciting. It was cool, though, that I got to work with S and that we talked with a lot of people.

After an hour and a half or so of that we stopped and then Elder N and I had to come home. Elder N wanted to stay as long as possible, so we ended up being late. We got down to the party one half hour after it started. I kind of dressed up, but Elder N didn't, couldn't, and didn't want to.

The whole thing went pretty good. We helped out with the food and stuff. It was a total mess, and chaos, BUT it was a lot of fun. Everything turned out pretty well.

Now it's the end of the day, and I'm pooped. I've been desensitized and I'm tired.
