HOME JOURNAL

I don't have to feel bad for doing what's right!

Saturday, Sep 14, 1996
Location: Halberstadt

Today was pretty good. Also pretty cold. We started off the morning by having W A give us back the Book of Mormon we gave him. He seemed mad or something. After that we got rejected by a few zurückkehrens. We visited P's around 12:00, because we were in the area. They really like it when we stop by. After that we stopped by Fam. K. We were lucky and were able to talk for an hour or so with Frau K. She and P will try and make it to Church tomorrow. I hope they can. We came home from K around 15:00 and had Mittags. We ended up staying home because Elder N needed to finish unpacking and what not.

I realized something this morning that will really help me out in life. I should have realized this in the MTC. I think the reason I was kind of miserable was because I let the other Elders make me feel bad for wanting to do what's right. Well, what I realized this morning is that I DON'T HAVE TO FEEL BAD FOR DOING WHAT IS RIGHT! If my comps have problems with me doing what is right, then too bad for them. I'm not going to sacrifice my values to make other people feel comfortable in not keeping the rules.
