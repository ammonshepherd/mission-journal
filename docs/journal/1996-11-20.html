Germany Hamburg, April 10, 1996 - April 4, 1998
HOME JOURNAL

I fixed my bike

Wednesday, Nov 20, 1996
Location: Lübeck

Today was very locker, laid back. I was bad and didn't wake up until 8am. Then I didn't do too much studying either. I'm so bad, I haven't been studying all that well. Anyhow! We had pancakes and I wrote my letter home. A two and a half pager. It took me an hour and a half to write. I hate writing letters. After that we went across the street and bought some bread. I brought my bike up so I could fix it. And that I did. Now the fender is fixed and the lights work. It really sucks trying to work on a bike, or anything for that matter, without the right tools. I'm going to get me some real tools one of these days. A pair of pliers, some real screwdrivers.

After that, Elder B cashed his check and I changed my American cash into Deutsch Marks. We then looked around for a while trying to find a Christmas present for Elder B's girlfriend.

Then we went to M's work. We did a lot of work on our fangs, and next week we'll come back and finish them. It was totally cool. I'm going to look into doing that when I get back. M is so awesome. He had a ton of work due tomorrow, but he insisted on letting us make ours today. He's a great guy.

After that we went to the church and changed into church clothes. GMK was cancelled and our appointment fell through, so we came home. That was pretty much all for today.
