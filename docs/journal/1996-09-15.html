Sunday in Quedlinburg

Sunday, Sep 15, 1996
Location: Halberstadt

Today went pretty well. Church and all went fine. We had Mittags by Schwesters B and M. I was stuffed again. After that we rushed to Bahnhof and barely caught the train to QLB. We had to go there and change the appointment with Herr B and Frau S from Tues. to another day, because of Golden Conference. We got a hold of Herr B, but ran out of time, so we didn't get to Frau S. We had mad an appointment with B N, but something came up so she left a note. After we visited B's we visited K's for an hour and a half or so. AFter them we visited N. We were there until 20:40. By then it was too lat to go by Frau S, so we caught the train home. Golden Conference king of screwed things up, but I'm still glad to go. Anyhow, it's late. I want to sleep.
