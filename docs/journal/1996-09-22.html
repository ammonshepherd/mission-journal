Germany Hamburg, April 10, 1996 - April 4, 1998
HOME JOURNAL

2:30am wake up call

Sunday, Sep 22, 1996
Location: Halberstadt

Today was an O.K. day. It started out really cruddy. At 2:30am I awoke with a start, and realized that I was scheduled to give a talk today. That was terrible, because I had totally forgotten about my talk until this morning at 2:30am. I went back to sleep and awoke again at 6:00. I then hurriedly took a shower, and then spent the next hour frantically trying to put together a talk. It was supposed to be 15 min, so I was really bummed. Needless to say what I had was crap and would only last about 7 min. I was blessed, though. A also gave a talk today. She did it in English with J translating, so it took quite a bit of time. There was also a visitor from Leipzig, so he also spoke. There was only enough time for them and K S who gave the first talk. So I really lucked out.

After church we helped Br. J put his Möbel in the storage room in the basement. Then we took off to Sch. M's for Mittags. We had a good time there. Just enough food. We also sang a few songs that hopefully help Sch. M feel the Spirit. After that we visited Sch. B and kids for a while. Hopefully they will seriously try to reactivate themselves. They were baptized about 3 years ago, but are now inactive. We just visited with them and got to know them a little bit. I hope we can get them active again, too.
