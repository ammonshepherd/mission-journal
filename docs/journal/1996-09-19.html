Mission Journal

Germany Hamburg, April 10, 1996 - April 4, 1998
HOME JOURNAL

Back to Halberstadt

Thursday, Sep 19, 1996
Location: Halberstadt

The past few days I've been in Braunschweig. I went to Golden Conference, it was pretty awesome. I wrote my letter home like it was my journal. I think it's a bit confusing the way I wrote it, but oh well. I had a lot of fun there. I'm glad to be back in Halberstadt, and be able to sleep in a real bed. I had to sleep on a mattress on the floor. It was terrible. The Braunschweig apartment sucks! I sure hope everything gets back to normal. Na ja.

Well, I'm back in Halberstadt now. It was a fun four days in Braunschweig. Today was the last day there. We had interviews with President and also a District Meeting with the Braunschweig district. After that Elders' V, J, N and I went and had lunch at the other Elders' apartment. Then it was time to go home. We missed the train that would have got us hom in time for English class because the but to get us to the train station was late. By the time we got home, we were an hour late for class. The ladies were still there, so we talked to them for a little while and then made an appointment to have English class next week.

Thanks for all the letters everyone. Thanks for the kool-aid too. It sounds like everybody is keeping really busy. Sorry if this letter doesn't make too much sense. I think everything should start getting a little back to normal.

I love you all. You are all a great inspiration for me.
