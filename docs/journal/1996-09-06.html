Mission Journal

Germany Hamburg, April 10, 1996 - April 4, 1998
HOME JOURNAL

A down pour

Friday, Sep 06, 1996
Location: Halberstadt

Today was different. We did some street contacting this morning and also had a District meeting. Right after D.Versammlung we rushed over to Fam. K. No one was home, so we left a note. Then we came back and had Mittags. I made Macaroni and cheese, with the paprika cheese. It tasted just a little bit better. I want to find a cheese that'll make it taste really good.

After Mittags we did some TzT in the Neu Bau area. While we were doing that, we gave out a Book of Mormon, we were surprised by a lady who opened the door with only a shirt and undies on, and we got caught in a down pour. The later was cool. The first was the best, and the middle I'm going to try and forget ever happened. The down pour was neat though. We could see a big bank of dark clouds coming towards us. It passed right over us and when it did it rained hard.

After TzT we did a few contacts. We got a hold of one. A family who lives on Rudolf-Virchow, I can't remember the name. Anyhow, we talked with the man and his son and I think his daughter, for a while. We gave them a BoM and almost started the 1st discussion. We ended up not giving it, but we got a ride home from the son. They are a cool family. We didn't get a fest appointment, but we can come back next weekend. Hopefully we can get a hold of them and they will have interest in the church.

I just wish and hope and pray with all my heart that they will see the truth and want it! Oh yeah, Frau B sent us a postcard to say she couldn't keep our appointment.

From the weekly letter home:

We found what I hope are potential investigators. We've ony met with them once, but it was a nice talk and we can come back. We talked to the man, his son, and his daughter. They seemed to have interest, and hopefully it is sincere. We couldn't make an appointment because the dad is very busy with work and is only home on the weekends, so we'll just have to try out luck at catching him and the rest of the family when they're all at home. We did give them a BoM though. Hopefully the will read it and pray about it. That was on Friday, Sept. 6, 1996.
