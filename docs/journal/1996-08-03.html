HOME JOURNAL

Thinking about missionary work makes the time fly

Friday, Aug 02, 1996
Location: Halberstadt

Today went pretty fast. This whole week went really fast. I'm so excited to buy Bro. D's bike. I can't wait to ride a bike again. Today went alright. It wasn't too hot today. If I stayed in the shade, I kept cool. There was also a little breeze that made it better. I did real well on studying the Discussions. I did 3 paragraphs today from the 6. Hauptgedanke. Hopefully, I'll have it done by Sunday evening when I teach it to Elder C.

I think I realized that when I try and keep my mind on missionary work and the discussions instead of home and J, then the time goes by faster. It's hard, though, to do that because Elder C is always talking about going home since he is going home in 2 months. I have still got 20, so it screws me up to think about home and the things I did and could do there. Like play the guitar and be mean to people, and think about J.
