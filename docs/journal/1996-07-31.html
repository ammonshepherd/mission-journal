Money troubles

Wednesday, Jul 31, 1996
Location: Halberstadt

I realized today that I've spent way too much money for Briefmarken (stamps) in the past. I've already spent 20DM this month and used them all today. From now on, I'll only write home and one other person. Today I wrote to TG, Brother G, the H's, K, and home, and Mormor and Papa. That used all of my Briefmarken. I also figured out that only 3DM are missing and not 80DM like I had thought at first. This month should go a lot better, because I've planned my budget a lot better. Hopefully, I'll come out even.

I also looked at Brother D's bike today. It's old, but in good condition. It needs new tubes and a front light and a tune up, and maybe a few more things. Other than that, it looks cool I'll buy it! Hopefully, for only 200DM to 250DM. I'll give Brother D my offer on Sunday.

That's about all!
