Germany Hamburg, April 10, 1996 - April 4, 1998
HOME JOURNAL

Zone Conference

Friday, Jul 19, 1996
Location: Halberstadt

Well, today was Zone Conference. WE had to leave the apartment at 5:45 to catch the Straßenbahn to the Bahnhof. The other Elders were there already when Elder C and I got there. Everything went fine, except for the HUGE luggage that we brought. It's Elder C's and had all the rest of his stuff. That was a pain in the you know what, but at least it had wheels, unlike Elder C's. We all got to the church in Hannover safely.

I got a letter from J (cousin) and got to see Elder R and K. They're doing well. The whole meeting went well. It was a lot like the MTC. A lot of talks and feeling very tired. After that we all came back. We got a few magazines while there. The Ensign, New Era, Der Stern, and some Church News. I said goodbye to Elder C for maybe the last time at the Bahnhof.

The ride home was pretty uneventful. We all had to walk home because there are no busses or S-Bahns after 20 Uhr. The bus does come a couple of times after 20 Uhr, but we missed it by 5 minutes and we could walk home before the next one came, so we did.

I finally did the dishes, too. Na ja, that's all that happened today.
