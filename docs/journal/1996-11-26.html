Pretty wasted day

Tuesday, Nov 26, 1996
Location: Lübeck

Tues. Today was pretty wasted. We left at 8:30 to catch the bus and then the train. But the bus was 20 minutes late so we missed the train too. We took the next train and ended up only a half hour late to Districts Meeting/Unterredungen. That went o.k.

We all went to Hamburg afterwards and had a Dönner. After that we came home. By the time we actually got home it was 19:00. We then made some calls and then visited Herr B. He still doubts. That's his main hindrance. After that we came home. It was pretty stressful at first, then turned o.k.
