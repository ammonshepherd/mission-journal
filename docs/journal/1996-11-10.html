HOME JOURNAL

A scary call from our friend

Sunday, Nov 10, 1996
Location: Lübeck

Today was one of those days! Unfortunately, I didn't get to see the church building here in Lübeck today because there was Stake Conference in Neumünster.

We left the apartment at 7:30am. We rode our bikes to Bahnhof and took the train to Neumünster. When we got to Neumünster, we had 10min until the program started. We ended up walking to the building, that took 20 minutes, after we asked a billion people how to get there. By the time we got there and found out which building it was in, we were an hour late.

After the Conference, we got a ride home with the M family. We had lunch at their place. It was fun and good. They're a cool family. After that we took the bus to Bahnhof and rode our bikes home. We then went by our appointment to teach a first, but it fell out. We did TzT instead. Then we rode over to I's. We were 45 minutes early, so we walked to the Hospital around the corner and visited a member there. She was in intensive care and doing pretty bad. We talked to her for a while then went back to I's. There we played Uno and gave her the stop smoking stuff. After that we went to N's. We had some Torte and tea. We talked and I got to know the family. They are really cool. We were there until 9:45pm. We rode our bikes home in the rain and made it by 10:20. At home was a phone call waiting for us. It was I. We called her back, and she told us she had tried to kill herself. We talked with her for a good half-hour, trying to help her. Satan is really working hard on her, making her feel unworthy and worthless. She had slit her arm, taken a lot of sleeping pills, and drunk a lot of alcohol. She sounded really bad. The feeling of hopelessness I had was terrible. I think I was inspired to have Elder B tell her a few things. I hope they helped. The whole experience sucked. She just felt so bad and I didn't really know what to do to help. After we said all that we could think of, we went to bed. Surprisingly I didn't feel too much. Just sort of calm. It's all totally weird. I really hope she pulls through.
