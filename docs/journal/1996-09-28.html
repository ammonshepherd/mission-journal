HOME JOURNAL

Mrs. Doubtfire...

Saturday, Sep 28, 1996
Location: Halberstadt

Today went pretty well. We read in the Book of Mormon with Bruder J. After that we came home and waited for J to call about the soup kitchen thing. We ended up not doing that. Instead we fixed the TzT Berichts and Kontakts and all that stuff. It's not done yet, but we got it started. We did that for a couple of hours until our appointment with K. That was hectic. The whole family was home, that was good. Herr K and Elder N took over P's project. They finished it and even let P do a few things. I mainly played with the kids. Then we had to stay and watch Mrs. Doubtfire. So we didn't get home until 10:30. I really hate it when we don't keep the rules. What can I do, though? Na, ja.
