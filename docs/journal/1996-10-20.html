Germany Hamburg, April 10, 1996 - April 4, 1998
HOME JOURNAL

An interesting primary program

Sunday, Oct 20, 1996
Location: Halberstadt

I had a good day today. Church went really well. Frau K came with P and D. They were there for Abendmahls and Sonntagschule. The kids definitely had a good time. I'm not too sure about Frau K, but I hope she felt the Spirit. The program was given by the primary kids, all 4 of them. It was pretty good. A small branch in a foreign land is a lot different from home. The program was video taped and a few pictures were also taken. Stuff like that would NEVER happen in the wards at home. C and P B also came to church.

After Church we took our Mittags. We had mashed potatoes and the Wurst that Sch. Z brought. We didn't eat the peas or peaches. After Mittags, I took a nice long nap. By then we had run out of time to visit Sch. B and Brd P. We couldn't visit Sch. M because she wouldn't open the door because her foot hurt too much to get up. Elder N had called to find out if we could visit.

So we stayed home the whole day. I filled out some TzT Berichts while Elder N organized a few things.

We also sang with Brd. J. It's not quite as fun as it was with Elder C or Elder C, because Elder N doesn't sing all that well. Elder C, Brd. J and I really had a couple of songs that sounded really good when we sang.

Today was a good day. I've written all that needs to be written. The day will come when I will be in a new city. Then it will turn into my old city, too. By then, my German better be good and a few more Hauptgedankes passed off.
