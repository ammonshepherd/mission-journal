Germany Hamburg, April 10, 1996 - April 4, 1998
HOME JOURNAL

P-Day and off to Braunschweig again

Wednesday, Oct 30, 1996
Location: Halberstadt

Today was OK. It was P-Day. We went shopping with Brd. P. We bought a bit of food. Before we went shopping I made pancakes. After shopping I wrote a letter home. It was three pages long. After that we went to the bank and cashed our check. Then we went home and changed our clothes and went out to do weight lifting. After we did that we played basketball with J. It was just J and us. We played 21 until dark. We then came home and sat around until English. We helped B and A with their Halloween stuff, and then when Frau S cancelled English Class because she was the only one again. J, U, and B came to make decorations and we ended up in Brd. J's apartment. J dropped us off at the train depot, and then we came here, to Braunscwheig, also known as Brunswick, my second home. It's late, I'm tired and hungry. I will now go to bed.
