Mission Journal

Germany Hamburg, April 10, 1996 - April 4, 1998
HOME JOURNAL

Teaching lessons

Tuesday, Jul 16, 1996
Location: Halberstadt

This day went pretty fast and good. Bruder P came early to say hello to Elder C and do some other things.

We then rushed off to the Bahnhof and caught the train to Quedlinburg. In Quedlinburg we visited Herr B. He and his wife said they promised to have breakfast for us. I didn't remember that but was grateful for the food all the same. We mostly just got to know Herr B a little better this time. I had found a ton of scriptures on prayer like he wanted, but I left that at home, so on the way to Quedlinburg I found a few real quick and gave those to him. I think we need to give him some kind of commitment. I think it's great and all that he wants to talk with the missionaries and that he's interested in finding out more. But he can learn all that stuff in Sunday school. I'll see what I can do.

After Herr B we did a little bit of Tür zu Tür without any luck. Then we came back to Halberstadt. When we got back to Halberstadt Elder C bought a Monatskarte and then we headed home for Mittags, that was about 15:00.

We then ate, and Elder C prepared for Institute. We were a little late getting there because the Bahn was 5 minutes late. It was also by Schwester B's instead of Schwester M's. B was there with all the other normals, and I could tell she was a little, actually, a whole lot mad at me. I know she thought I was the one that called Pres. B. Unfortunately, we didn't have time to talk then.

After Institute we got a ride with J to Dr. W's where we had an appointment. That went pretty well. We taught him the first and he took the Book of Mormon and said he would pray. He asked us what our goal was, so Elder C said to teach people the Lektions and when they find out our church is true, to baptize them. I thought it could have been said a little bit better, but what do I know. Anyhow, Dr. W said he had no intention of getting baptized, but he would read and pray about the BoM.

So that all went pretty good, and then we headed home. B was practicing the piano in the chapel, so I went in and talked with her. She is really upset that someone called Pres. and didn't even talk to her about anything first. I just hope she believes that I really wasn't the one who called Pres. The thing that is really tragic is the way some of the members are gossiping. They say that B and Elder C did things, and she is the reason he left and she's a missionary chaser and all kinds of stuff. It's really sad, because now she hates the ward. There is nothing I can do though, because it's not my problem. I try not to worry about it for that same reason. I can just pray that all goes well.
