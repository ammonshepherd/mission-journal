We rode our bikes like the devil

Thursday, Nov 14, 1996
Location: Lübeck

If I didn't have such a good outlook on everything, this day would have really sucked. This morning we missed two buses to the train station. We had to ride our bikes like the devil there. On the way I ran over a beer can. Seems like it would be no problem, but nooo, not day. The can stuck on my tire and ended up busting my fender! Now both lights are broke and so is my fender. And I don't have any time to fix them. We caught the train and we were even on time for Districts meeting. That was pretty fun. It was good to mess around with Elder R. Elder C is pretty cool, too. After that, we came back to Lübeck from Rahlstedt. Luckily our bikes were still there. We then took the bus to our eating appointment with Sch. F. That was o.k. She's a cool lady. After that we met with R. She's going to make it! We finished up the baptismal program and had a talk with her a little about self-esteem. She made it today without a cigarette and she'll make it from now on. She's a little scared because she'll be living a whole new life and what not. We comforted her the best we could. I'm a little scared too, to give her the Holy Ghost. All will go well.

After that we picked up our bikes and rode home, then went straight to Sportabend. Today was awesome. There was only six people altogether. We played a game of three on three basketball. It made fun. After that we came home. That was all for today. I also passed off the second!
