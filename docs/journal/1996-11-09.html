Where I forget the 1st discussion

Saturday, Nov 09, 1996
Location: Lübeck

Today was totally awesome. We got quite a bit done. We translated the 15 steps to quit smoking, after we had read the manual. It was really cheesy, but has really good ideas. After that we had lunch. A huge plate full of mashed potatoes. After that we visited a few contacts. We even taught one the 1st discussion. That's what made today so awesome. The man, Herr B, is just plain ready. He was feeling the Spirit STRONG and often. The only problem is that he doubts, and he lets his doubts be stronger than the Spirit. The only thing that didn't go well was that I forgot the 1st discussion so Elder B did it all. I used the pamphlet or book or whatever it's called, to do the 4th Hauptgedanke. I was so mad at myself because I have let myself forget it.

After that we found a Rossman that was still open and I bought some gel and some toothpaste. I'm going to try the gel for a while to see if it won't help my hair stay in place. We also bought some Vitamin C pills for I and the 15 steps to no smoking. After that we did TzT for 3 hours on the streets I picked out. The exact street I picked out was unfinished apartment buildings, but the other streets in the area had tons of people. We did TzT in the are for 3 hours. We got a few appointments made and we spoke with a ton of people.

After that we came home.
