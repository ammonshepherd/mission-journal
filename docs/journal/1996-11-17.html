I hope my future kids are cool

Sunday, Nov 17, 1996
Location: Lübeck

It never gets better. I'll start at the beginning.

We all went to Church, and I was lost. Everyone was new. I didn't know too many. I was totally surprised, too. R came to church with her friends from Rohstock. That took a lot of guts. Church went pretty well.

All four of us Elders were invited to eat at Bishop w's. That was a total blast. We ended up being there for three hours or so. We took the other Elders to the Bahnhof, then came back for another half hour or so. I had tons of fun playing with the kids. They have one boy about 10 and two girls one almost 8 and the other about 6. They're so awesome. I love kids. I can't wait to have my own, and I hope they're cool.*

After Mittags at W's we got dropped off here and were going to plan the rest of the 2 1/2 hours of the day. We called Pres. and he told us R can be baptized in 4 weeks at the earliest. We then called R. she is MESSED! Now she doesn't want to be baptized, she's trying to kill herself again, and she's started smoking again. She's waiting for the right reason though. She doesn't believe that abortion is a sin at all. She was saying all kinds of weird stuff. She won't pray and she wants to give up. She's cookoo. All this stuff is messing up the work. We need to find some more people, hopefully normal people. This whole thing is tearing Elder B up. We'll see how everything turns out in a couple of months.

I have six and they are more awesome and cool than I could ever have imagined.
