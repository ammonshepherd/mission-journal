Do you have anything to do with Mormons?!

Monday, Nov 18, 1996
Location: Lübeck

Today was totally wonderful. We did TzT, which actually wasn't all that good, but when is it ever. After that came lunch and then our visit with R. I was very surprised! It went totally awesome! As I look back, I know now that I was totally inspired. I said whatever popped into my head and it was what she needed. We talked for a long time, and at the end we weren't sure if she was going to give up or stick it out and be baptized. But I felt calm and OK.

After we were done with R, we went to Familie L. They are the Russian family. We taught the husband and the wife the first. It was awesome. They just soaked it up. They are even coming to Church on Sunday, and they're going to drive us! It was just an awesome night. We even got to watch a little of Blood Sport while waiting for Frau L to take care of some stuff.

For the icing on the cake, we visited Herr B. This man is soo AWESOME! It was funny, though. He said to us right off, I read in the brochure, Joseph Smith's testimony, something about Mormons. Do you have anything to do with Mormons? We said yes, we are the Mormons! Then he said, If I knew that at the first, I wouldn't have let you in. He had just heard a lot of things, but we set him straight. We just had an all out good talk with him. He still has problems believing that God will answer his prayer, but he knows what needs to be done.

We also gave him a Book of Mormon, which let us get 1, 2, 3. And best of all, R called and said she is ready to be baptized.
