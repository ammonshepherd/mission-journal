It was all good

Saturday, Jul 27, 1996
Location: Halberstadt

Today went pretty quick. We spent a lot of time today making a three layer cake for Elder H's birthday. It was really sweet, but tasted really good. It was big, too. Elder H was really surprised when we brought it down with us to do numbers. It was cool.

I also got a letter from C today. She wrote it all by herself. That made my day.

What made my day not so good was that my feet were killing me at the end of today. My shoes are really messing up my heels.

We talked with a man from Ethiopia, and he said that he will come to church tomorrow. Hopefully! He's living in the refugee camp south of Halberstadt. I didn't even know there was one. Anyhow, this day went alright, in fact "it was all good."
