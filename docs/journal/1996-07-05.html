More old ladies

Friday, Jul 05, 1996
Location: Halberstadt

This day we finished the second building full of old ladies. Then we did a little bit more on the other street, door to door that is. Not much luck at all. After Mittags essen we finished up the last street and tried to visit old contacts until 18:00, we had an eating appointment with Sister M. The whole day, after lunch especially, I felt real sick. I think it was lunch, fried spaghetti. I didn't eat too much at Sister M's. We were there for a couple of hours, looking at pictures. It was raining really hard on the way home. I'm glad I had a Regenschild. Even though I did, a lot of stuff got wet.
