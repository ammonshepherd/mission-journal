It rained all day!

Tuesday, Aug 13, 1996
Location: Halberstadt

Today was a WET day. We rode bike to Quedlinburg today. That was no problem really, but I'll get to that. The biggest problem was that it rained all morning, all day, and is raining now at 9:45pm. It rained nonstop! So after 20 minutes of riding we were soaked. Now comes the problem of riding to QLB. Elder C in his infinite wisdom decided to pick a better and perhaps safer route to drive. He was once again wrong! Granted that scenery and everything was beautiful. But I hate riding 5 km on cobble stone!!!!

Today we drove through farm land on cobble stone and it was terrible. We also drove on roads like you can find in the desert back home, parts of this road were pure mud. Boy was it great.

So, in QLB we visited Herr B. We just wanted to see if he was better and out of the hospital, but he made us come in. His wife got ticked at us because we came during "Quiet time," a German tradition. Herr B didn't care though. Oh well.

After that we met with A, and we'll simply try to reach him another time because he was on his way out with a friend. AFter that we visited "the old lady," Frau B. That went like it always does, ending with her giving us a kiss on the cheek.

Then it was time to ride back home. This time wa a lot easier. It was still raining, so we got even wetter if such a thing is possible. When we got home we changed and got ready for Institute. That went all normal. On the way home we bought Dönners because today was Dönner day. We ate that with real American Jello. So all in all, today went ok, considering that I was wet for half the day.
