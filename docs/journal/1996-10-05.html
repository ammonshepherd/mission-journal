General Conference, day 1!

Saturday, Oct 05, 1996
Location: Halberstadt

Today was pretty awesome. Elder N and I visited Brd. J in the Morgen. He's doing better, reading wise. AFter that we came home and then rushed to the Bahnhof. We tried to catch the Straßenbahn, but we missed it by a couple of minutes. We tried to get there in time by taking the later Straßenbahn, but we missed catching the train by a few minutes also. We waited around the Bahnhof for an hour until the next train. Then we came to Hannover.

We were able to see the first session of Conference, the Saturday morning, just after we got to the Stake Center. It was a cool session. I just enjoyed it all. Listening to the prophet and the apostles, and in English.

After that we came to the Wohnung. We played cards and what not. It was a good day, I thought.
