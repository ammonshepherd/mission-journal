Life sucks when you mix girls in with it

Monday, Jul 01, 1996
Location: Halberstadt

Today was a busy day. We left the apartment early so that we could catch the Zug nach Quedlinburg (train to Quedlinburg) and be at Herr B's at 10:30. We got there just in time. By the way, I asked for the tickets and did it perfectly. Anyhow, we go to Herr B's and there was a note on the door that said his wife had gotten sick, so he was at the Krankenhaus (hospital) with her, and that we should come back next Monday, same time. After that we went and did Tür zu Tür on Wall Straße again. We made two appointments with two girls, age range from 15-18. That'll be fine and dandy if they join the Church. Anyhow, while we were doing Tür zu Tür it started to rain. And neither of us had thought it would, so neither of us had brought a Regenschild (umbrella) or rain coat. We only got a little bit wet. For the most part, we ran from building to building and tried to find shelter in the doorways.

After we got back to Halberstadt we checked on a contact that said we could come by today. For some reason I wasn't surprised when no one was there. We did street contacting on the way home. After Mittags Pause (lunch), which we finally took at 15:00-16:00, we headed over to Schwester B's. We met Brown Mantel Man, and then B met with us. We talked for only a short while.

Schwester B ended up not being there, so B came with Elder C and I to finish up Am Sommerbad. We did so and got a Termin for Freitag (Friday). The whole day today, I was thinking about how I could approach the subject that we spend too much time with B. I know we need to talk about it because the problem is still here. I think Elder C likes B more than he should, so takes every chance and excuse he can to talk with her for as long as he can. Everything in me is screaming that what they are doing and what I'm allowing them to do is wrong. It is, too. I looked in the White Handbook and the "Anleitung" just to make sure. They both say and stress that we are never to be alone with someone of the opposite sex. I interpret that as no getting rides, which would make us alone in a car; no eating food in the basement of the Church; and not letting Elder C and B sit in the chapel until 9:30pm alone. Unfortunately, I don't think Elder C sees it the same way. I don't want to say anything because last time we talked about it obviously didn't do anything. I know I should though, but I just hate to. I really do HATE being in this situation. It feels like ever since I've been on a mission, I've had to deal with this problem in some way. It really BITES. I have no idea why this challenge or this problem keeps coming before me. Maybe it's to prepare me for later on in my mission.

Oh well, what can I say, what can I do? Life SUCKS! when you try to mix girls in with it. ESPECIALLY while on a mission. I want to just leave the problem alone for two months until Elder C leaves, but I don't know if that will just make it worse or not. Time will tell. I just wish I didn't have to be the one who has to live through it. Oh well, enough complaining.
