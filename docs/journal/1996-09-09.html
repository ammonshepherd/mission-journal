Mission Journal

Germany Hamburg, April 10, 1996 - April 4, 1998
HOME JOURNAL

Two ices today!

Monday, Sep 09, 1996
Location: Halberstadt

Today was really cool. This morning we sent Elder C's bike off. Herr A, the Hausmeister, let us borrow his hand wagon, so it was easy to take the huge box to Post. After Post we helped out Frau F. She gave us two ices today. After Mittags I made cookies to take with us to Brd. J's while Elder C planned for Institute. We had fun just shooing the breeze at Brd. J's. After that we went by on a few contacts. Not so viel luck with that. We stopped by K's around 19:30. At first only Frau K and the kids were home. We talked and played. I gave the kids an American dollar. Best of all I got to play the guitar. It was awesome. I've pretty much forgotten how to play. Hopefully I can re-learn them when I get back. That's in two years, so I'll worry about it then. Anyhow, around 20:30 or so Herr K came home, so we talked with the both of them for a little while longer. Until 21:15 to be exact. Herr K gave us some bread and then we were on our way home.

Meeting with K to night was another answer to prayer. By the time I'm done here, they'll be ready for baptism; only with the Lord's help.

From the weekly letter home:

Monday was a pretty cool day, too. We sent Elder C's bike home. It was a lot of pain to send it home, and personally i don't think his bike was worth it, but oh well. Hopefully, or my bike BETTER be in good condition when I get home so that I won't need to send my mission bike home. Although it is pretty nice. It was a lot of stress relieved when we finally got it sent off. He ended up having to make his own box, 120cm by 60cm by 60cm. That's BIG! Oh well! at least it's all over with.

Also cool on Monday, was that we met with our only real investigator family. There is the mom and dad and two kids. A nine year old boy and a six year old gir. They are really cool. They have good ideals for the kids. Not too much T.V. because of what's on it, they want them to be religious, they won't allow them to ride a motorcycle, stuff like that. Only problem is that they smoke a lot and the man drinks. By the way their name is K. Anyhow, if we cold start making progress with them in the discussions, which I think we will, then I know that they can overcome whatever problems they might have. They'll make awesome members! Anyhow, on Monday night we met with them. I played with the kids a lot and gave them each an American dollar. They thought that was pretty cool. We didn't leave there until 9:15pm. We had been there for only two hours. We barely got home before 9:30pm, which is curfew time.

There was also something else that happened on Monday, as we were walking to Familie K. We stopped and talked to the Halberstädter Drunks! There are probably more than the two we talked to, but I've seen them more often. We talked to them for a little bit about America and then that was all. One of them gave us a half-full bottle of apple juice that smelled like beer. As we shook the hand of the other one, he tipped over and fell on his side. He cut his head, but probably didn't feel it because he was so drunk! We helped him up and I told them to quit drinking alcohol. Maybe one of these days they will. Anyhow, that's my second experience with drunks now.
