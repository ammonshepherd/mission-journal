Why do I have all these zits!?

Wednesday, Oct 23, 1996
Location: Halberstadt

Today went blah. We were late getting to Braunschweig for Districts meeting, so half of the people left before we got there. We had it anyhow. Instead of going to the park to play football with the six of us who stayed, we played in front of the church. That was pretty fun. I dove once and got my pants all dirty. I also got all sweaty. After football, Elder N, Elder H, Elder S, and I went to exchange Elder N's coat. Elder N also bought some juggling balls. AFter that we got Dönners. They weren't quite as good as Halberstadt's. We then went to Toys R Us to look for presents. After that we came home to Halberstadt. We had English class, and Brd. J started moving things in. Tomorrow starts the real stuff. Then Brd. J will be officially moved in. I hope it all turns out for the best.

I started letting Elder N get to me . I won't let it happen though. He's not better than me! Even though I think he acts like it. That's the impression I get at least. Lots of things were eating at me. I kept them in and overpowered them. They no longer bug me.

My throat is really bugging me. Why do I have all of these zits? Why won't they go away? I wish they would at least quit itching. I hate it!!!!!!

I only got one letter written today. I had no time!
