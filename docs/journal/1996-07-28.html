Door to door and deja vu

Sunday, Jul 28, 1996
Location: Halberstadt

Today went pretty well. The guy from Ethiopia didn't come to church, but Sister F did. We can visit her tomorrow.

After church we had dinner at D's. It was all four Elders, J, K, B and U also. We had good stuff. I was full when I left. We also played a game, like bluff. That was pretty fun.

After that we, Elder C and I, tried to reach some contacts and less actives. We got ahold of a few, and we can go back to one or two of those. After that we did some door to door on Ernst-Henkel Str. I had de-ja-vu. We were talking to a lady who had spoken with the missionaries a few years ago. It all just seemed like it happened before. Especially when she asked me or rather said that I was German. Also when I bore my testimony that Heavenly Father really does hear our prayers and answer them. She said she would come to church during Christmas time, but that she can't believe anymore on God because of all the things that have happened to her in her life. Na ja, hoffentlich etwas gut später in ihre Leben passieren wird. That's all folks!
