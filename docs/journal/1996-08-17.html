Yelled at by young and old

Saturday, Aug 17, 1996
Location: Halberstadt

Today was short and sweet. Sweet because I had a lot of chocolates at Bruder J's. Short because it went by real fast. And short and sweet because it was a simple day, nothing too exciting. We got yelled at by a twelve year old girl. She kept yelling at us to leave her in peace, she would also tell us to shut-up. She also threatened to beat us up, have people beat us up, and call the police on us. She was a little devil. Na ja, what does she know!

Some old lady yelled at us to get out of the building we were doing TzT in. That of course is normal. Hopefully everything will go OK next week. I sure wish we could find someone with real interest that will want to get baptized. Not really because I want a baptism, but to show me that there really is a point to what I'm doing! Na ja!
