Germany Hamburg, April 10, 1996 - April 4, 1998
HOME JOURNAL

Golden Conference!

Tuesday, Sep 17, 1996
Location: Halberstadt 
Well, Today, Tues, was Golden Conference. After three months in Germany we go to the Mission home and then we become junior comps. So we stayed over night in Braunschweig to make the trip easier.

So today's activities are as follows. Elder V and I woke up at 5:00am and got ready to go to Golden Conference. We rode the train to Hannover where we were met by Elder K. Then we took the ICE (fastest train in Germany; 200km) to Hamburg. There we met Elder R. Then the four of us went to the mission home together. Elder R was already there, so as soon as we got there we got started. The Golden Conference was just like a Zone Conference, but only with my MTC district. It was really cool. We learned a lot, and it was neat to see all the guys from the MTC again. After Conference we all left back for home. Two cool things happened on the way home. One was that we got pizza from Pizza Hut. The second was even better. On the train from Hannover to Braunschweig Elder V and I talked to two people about the Church and other stuff the whole way. We did it mostly in English, too. The lady that Elder V talked to will call him and make an appointment to talk with him and his comp more about the Church. She seemed really interested. They guy I talked to lives in Berlin. I gave him the number for the Hamburg Mission and told him that they have the number for the missionaries in Berlin if he wants to get ahold of them. I found out yesterday that on Thursday we have interviews with Pres. My comp and I were planning on going home Wed. afternoon. But since we would have to come back to Braunschweig for the interviews, we are just going to stay here. So we came to Braunschweig on Monday afternoon, and are staying here until Thursday afternoon.
