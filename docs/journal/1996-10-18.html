I make A LOT of pancakes

Friday, Oct 18, 1996
Location: Halberstadt

Today went pretty well. This morning in Braunschweig, I made pancakes. I was really screwing up, but they ended up good. There was just a whole lot. I timesed the recipe by 8, with it having originally 3/4 cups of flour. So there was quite a bit. I really screwed up on the syrup. I tried to thicken it with Speisestärker. It didn't work. Elder J made more, and everything turned out fine.

Zone Conference went really well. I didn't have to give a talk. It was a long one with lunch in the middle. After Conference, Elder N and I went to Karstadt and bough wax-jackets. It cost me 200DM. I really like it! I just can't buy anything for the rest of my mission now.

We got back home with no problems. I got a letter from T. He sent me a planner, and a picture.

I'm really tired, so I'm going to go to bed.
