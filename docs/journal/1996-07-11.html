Girl problems

Thursday, Jul 11, 1996
Location: Halberstadt

This morning I'm really bumming. My soul is burdened. The situation I've always seemed to be in since my mission SUCKS!! Why do my comps always have to be in love or what not with girls while they're here. MENSCH! Elder C and B is what I'm talking about. I had thought he was getting better, but I was wrong. Yesterday he was aching to get out and do something, he wanted to go to the garden. I thought he just wanted to be outside. He really wanted to be with B. It pisses me off! We always have to do crap with B. She always has to be with us. Yesterday, she drove us to S. B.'s garden and then to Quedlinburg, and then we came back and ate Dönners in the Keller again. We were down there again until 10:30. I hate that. When we do that, we don't have time to plan for the next day, so we just run around like chickens with our heads cut off. To me it feels like we're not being very productive. I feel that every time we are with B we are breaking the rules. I HATE THIS!

Yesterday, I just finally gave in. I figured Elder C isn't going to listen so what the heck! I just feel so bad inside because we keep breaking rules so B and Elder C can be together.

I have no idea what to do. Talking about it didn't work last time. I don't think it'll work any more. The impression I get is that Elder C thanks he only has one month left, so he can do whatever he wants. Well I'm sick of breaking rules so he and B can be together. To tell the truth, I really can't wait until next month. Then, hopefully, this problem will be over.
