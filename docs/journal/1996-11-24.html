Germany Hamburg, April 10, 1996 - April 4, 1998
HOME JOURNAL

3 friends at Church to hear my talk

Sunday, Nov 24, 1996
Location: Lübeck

Wow! Everyday is different and weirder than the last. Today we were supposed to get a ride to Church with our investigator family who come from Russia. It should have been no surprise, but they weren't home when we got there. We ended up calling a taxi so that I could get there on time to give my talk. We got there just before church started. Church went o.k. My talk went o.k. M C, R and Frau W were all at Church today. It wasn't too good in Priesthood, though. M was there and the lesson just happened to do with the three degrees of glory and a lot of other deep stuff. It was kind of funny. In the middle of the discussion he stopped us and asked us to speak in German so that he could understand. We should have went to a different room and taught him the third. But on well. We meet tomorrow, and I hope everything will go well.

After Church we had lunch at S's. They've been married two years and still have no kids. We had a good meal! With Elderberry juice. We had a good time there.

Then we went by on a few contacts. We got a hold of Herr O and read 3 Nephi 11 with him. He'll take some work, but I think he has potential. After that we stopped by Familie L, the Russians. They thought it was on Saturday and not Sunday. They had waited for us for an half-hour. So after we got that all cleared up, we chatted with them for a bit then came home.
