Germany Hamburg, April 10, 1996 - April 4, 1998
HOME JOURNAL

My piano playing was just as bad as their singing

Friday, Jul 12, 1996
Location: Halberstadt

This day was a little more productive in my opinion. I picked out a new street for us to do door to door. We got to house 26 with almost everyone home, but they all had no interest. At house 26 we met a girl, 14, called B, we taught her the first right there. That was cool. She took the Book of Mormon and said she would read it and pray about it and Joseph Smith. I sure hope she does, and gets an answer.

Today I was pretty bummed about my language. Almost everyone I met said they couldn't understand me. It made me kind of depressed. Well, it's only my first month, I"ve got 21 left to work on my language.

After we taught B the first discussion, we came home and I made ravioli with extra noodles and tomato paste, so that there would be enough for the both of us. I used too much tomato paste, so it tasted kind of strong, but still pretty good. Then we prepared for Missionarsabend.

After our appointment with the man and wife on Am Sommerbad we ran around with B and then came back. The appointment though, didn't go all that well. They didn't really have all that much interest. They mainly just want to know about our church.

The Misisonarsabend went pretty well. When I played the piano, I messed up a lot, but it sounded just as good as the members singing. That's about all that happened.
