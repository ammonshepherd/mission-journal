Mission Journal

Germany Hamburg, April 10, 1996 - April 4, 1998
HOME JOURNAL

Pretty well and pretty hectic

Thursday, Sep 05, 1996
Location: Halberstadt

Today went pretty well. It was also pretty hectic. We did a lot of running around while doing street contacting. We found a bunch of stuff for Elder C about sending his baggage home or wherever he needs it to go. We met with P B at 11:00. We just talked with him for a while, asked him how he was going. He had just gotten out of the hospital yesterday. He had some kind of operation on his back. We took him a Rittersport also.

After that we had Mittags, and Elder C prepared for his talk. Then we had planned to do some contacts, but the first one we did ended up lasting from 17:00 or so until 18:00. It was the man Elder C and I had talked to before and where we met Herr F. This time it was the guy who lived there and his other friend. We talked with them for a while, then they made me call Herr F. I just asked him how it was going. They wanted me to do it so I could talk to him in English. They all had a big kick out of it.

Anyhow, we were there for an hour or so and then went to Fam. B. We had Abendbrot and read in the scriptures. They had Kinder Eier for us. My Überraschung was a weeny cat singing to a lady cat on a wall. I had bought another Überraschung Eier earlier and it was a cheap prize too.

Elder C is getting all ready to go, packing and making a box for his bike. It's stressful and hectic when a missionary goes home. Especially from Halberstadt. That's all folks!
