HOME JOURNAL

Today was HOT!

Thursday, Aug 01, 1996
Location: Halberstadt

What a way to start a month! Today was HOT! we did a lot of door to door. We also saw some guys pick up a tractor that had tipped over. I spent 1,19DM on Fanta pop and orange juice, because I was dying of thirst. I was so thirsty I had cotton mouth. I also payed back my PG and bought a Monatskarte. So the first day this month I spent 83,19DM. What a bummer! We also helped a guy carry some carpet home. We saw him on our way home so we were still in suits. By the time we got done with helping him, I was soaked to the bone in sweat, and my forearms were so messed up I couldn't make a tight fist. Other than all that, it went by fine!
