Mission Journal

Germany Hamburg, April 10, 1996 - April 4, 1998
HOME JOURNAL

Lots of fast walking

Thursday, Sep 26, 1996
Location: Halberstadt

Today went pretty well. I actually did all the study things this morning. I even did the Anleitung. I did it alone, but at least I did it. I also practiced the guitar this morning. I don't really want to sing one of my songs for the Talent Abend, but thy're the only ones I can. I think I'm just afraid that nobody will like them.

Anyhow, after that we went by the Landrats Amt and took care of something for Elder A. We also go our pictures. I got most of mine back, 30 out of 36 or so. Not bad. The pictures were pretty good too.

We then went really quickly to Fam. K and dropped a note off, telling about and inviting them to Talent Abend. Then we rushed back to K. We had to wait a little while for Frau and Herr K to show up, but they eventually did. There we helped P make his mobil thing. We did a good job, and got all the planets cut out. (It was a mobil of the solar system.) We then came home and waited for J to call. He never did. At about 19:00, Elder N really wanted to drop a note by Fam. E, so we did that then. We did that then dropped by Schw. B. We got home right at 9:30. All in all it was a good day. My foot hurts from walking fast.
