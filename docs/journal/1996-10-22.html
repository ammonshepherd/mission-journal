Mission Journal

Germany Hamburg, April 10, 1996 - April 4, 1998
HOME JOURNAL

Apartments, Institute, Wolfenbüttel, but no VW's

Tuesday, Oct 22, 1996
Location: Halberstadt

When I think back on this day, I think that it was pretty good. For most of the day, Elder N and I cleaned out what is now Brd. J's apartment. We got everything cleaned out. We visited Brd. P in the hospital. We met up with Sch. & U P before we got into the hospital. After that we came home and Elder N finished Institute and I finished the apartment. I just vacuumed the floor real good, got rid of the 5 years of dust that collected over the years.

After that it was Institute. Brd. J and J were the only ones on time and went down a while before Elder N. I told Brd. J that his apartment was all cleaned out. He almost went berserk! He and J started right then to move stuff from the Keller up. They got a cabinet thing for the kitchen put in by the time everyone else came.

Institute was crazy. We had it at the church because Sch. B is still in Blankenburg. Those that were there were J, U, B, A, Brd J, Elder N, and me. It was pretty wild. U & B laughing the whole time and what not. It was fun. After Institute, I helped Brd. J move his refrigerator into his kitchen. Then J gave us a ride to Bahnhof because we brought the Wolfenbüttel Elders a small oven thing. We got to Wolfenbüttel all right.

That was pretty much all for the day.
