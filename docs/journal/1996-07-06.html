More Quedlinburg

Saturday, Jul 06, 1996
Location: Halberstadt

On this day, we spent some time in Quedlinburg. First off, we spent some time figuring out what to do for Primary. Then we tried to do some street contacting. We did that for about an hour and a half, with not much luck. I made Knödel, rice, green beans, and gravy for lunch, with cool-aid. Then we did some more street contacting until the train left for Quedlinburg. In Quedlinburg we spent 2 hours listening to Frau B. She's the real old lady that gives kisses. A funny thing that happened there was she gave us a cup of water. My cup was filthy and the water was brown because there was dirt in it. I didn't want to drink it, so I poured it in a pot on the stove behind me. After that we did some Tür zu Tür and made a few appointments. Then we cam home. I did the dishes and prepared a cheesy lesson for primary.

I took lots of pictures and titled the sequence, A Day In The Life Of A Missionary

Here is the story of a missionary. Every morning he wakes up at exactly 6am.

He walks through the living/family/whatever room, on his way to the bathroom, where he takes a shower sitting down, because the shower head isn't fixed to the wall, and because the bathtub isn't covered. (I didn't think you want to see a picture of the bathroom, so I didn't take one.)

He walks into the kitchen after his bath/shower, and thinks up a nutritious breakfast that will give him energy for the whole day.

He looks out the window to see the beautiful world awakening. It's difficult, though! The window is covered with flying ANTS! After sucking them all up with the vacuum cleaner, he's ready for Früstuck!

He looks at the sink and stove, and is glad he did the dishes the night before. Then he thinks... what should I ead.

Maybe I'll have some juice, or maybe some milk that doesn't ever need to be refrigerated and has a shelf life of two months. Mmmm, mmmm, German cuisines. (I'm mad this didn't turn out. I think the aim on the camera is screwed up too.)

On his way out to preach the gospel, he passes by his favorite car. The LDS Trabi. (Look close at the license plate.)

Sometimes he even passes by the horse meat shop. (Some people really eat that, because they really sell it.)

After a good rain, he sees plenty of snails and slugs on the street and fences. (Crumy picture.)

On this particular day, he's traveling to Quedlinburg. This is the train station. (This is actually in Quedlinburg.)

Some neat scenery on the way there.

Wheat field with Halberstadt in the background. Taken from the train.

Ah! Beautiful Quedlinburg. (Rathouse, Fusgangerzone, chapel, other buildings.)

He walks through the many interesting streets.

Today he's doing door to door. (A church steeple from the porch of a house.)

He sees many strange things. (That's a real bird!)

After a long days work, it's back to the train station.

And more pretty scenery. (That's Elder C.)
