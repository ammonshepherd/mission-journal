Mission Journal

Germany Hamburg, April 10, 1996 - April 4, 1998
HOME JOURNAL

A triple 1st lesson!

Sunday, Jul 14, 1996
Location: Halberstadt

Today was pretty cool. Church went pretty normal. Familie D was there, they are back from vacation in America. They are the ones that lived in Mesa.

My prayers were answered. I got help with the problem between B and Elder C. Br. D and Br. S had a talk with us, all four missionaries, about getting involved with members problems. Hopefully this talk will solve the problem.

After Church all of the Elders had Mittags bei U P. It was good. B had shown up near the end. I gave the spiritual thought, then we all left. B was going to give us a ride home, but the other Elders were going in a different direction, so it would have been just B and us like usual. Fortunately, one of U's guests, I forget her name, she's from Hannover, anyhow she had stuck her head out the window and reminded us that we weren't allowed to do that. So we walked home. I was glad. As we turned the corner for home, I saw B parking in front of the Church. I thought everything was going to go as they usually did, but B instead went to her garden, and didn't even wave to us, that I saw.

After Elder C brushed his teeth and I figured out the streets we were going to do Tür zu Tür on, we left. We didn't have too much luck, in fact none at all. We did Tür zu Tür until it was time for us to leave for our appointment. We walked across half the town. My feet were so sore and I was very tired. Anyhow, the appointment went really well. We klingel'ed by P and nobody answered. Then we klingel'ed the name under hers and we talked with a man, but he didn't seem too interested, but then he let us in, or his wife did. Then she went and got Frau P, who we thought wasn't there. In the end we ended up teaching all three the first discussion and giving Frau P and Herr and Frau S both a Book of Mormon. We also have an appointment for this Thursday, an eating appointment even. All in all, it went pretty well. Even though I did screw up the 5th Hauptgedanke really bad.
