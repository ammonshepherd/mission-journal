Mission Journal

Germany Hamburg, April 10, 1996 - April 4, 1998
HOME JOURNAL

New companion

Thursday, Sep 12, 1996
Location: Halberstadt

Today was a very busy day. I spent the night, Wed. night, in Braunschweig. Wednesday was just a mess. I won't write about Wed., mostly because I forgot. I wrote most of what happened today in my letter home, so I wont' write too much in here. Just to say that I'm really tires and I've had enough of riding on buses and trains.

From the weekly letter home:

The day is about half way over. This morning Elder C left with Elder J to Hamburg. Elder J was Elder V's companion here in Braunschweig. So Elder V and I are here in Braunschweig waiting for our new companions to come. The weirdest thing is that Elder V was in the same district as me in the MTC, so we are both Goldens (Greenies)! Both of our comps. aren't coming to Braunschweig until 17:00, so we spent the whole day together, Elder V and me. We actually went out and about, too. There was a double transfer in another part of the Braunschweig district. It was actually an opening of a city. Anyhow, Elder V had to show the two new Elders where they lived. I'll tell it again in a clearer way. Two Elders came to open a city, that is in the Braunschweig District. Elder V knows where the apartment is, where they're going to stay, so we had to show them where that was. We also had to buy a table cloth. That was pretty fun. We also visited the Dom church here in Braunschweig. It was really neat. Underneath the chapel part is a catacomb type thing. There was about 20 caskets under there with real dead people in them. The caskets were really ornate and fancy. I got a couple of pictures too. 

Right now I'm waiting for my new comp. Elder N. When he gets here, we will both go to Halberstadt, and then hopefully everything will go back to normal.

If this letter has been real confusing, sorry. Now you know what I feel like. It's a strange feeling having a good friend and comp. go home, and then getting a new one.

Well, I'm home in Halberstadt now. It was a long day today. My new comp. Elder N seems pretty cool so far. He hasn't beat me up, threatened me, or forced me to break rules or anything like that, so it's all cool. I'm really tired now so I close this letter.

Thanks everyone for writing to me. I know this church is true, and I know that I am really blessed to be born in the Church.

Locked in the basement crypt of the Braunschweig Dom!
