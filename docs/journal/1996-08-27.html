Spiel des Lebens!

Tuesday, Aug 27, 1996
Location: Halberstadt

Today was a busy day. First off this morning, we waited in an office for an hour or so, for Elder C to get his Visa thing renewed so that he is allowed to stay in Germany until he leaves in a month. After that we did some street contacting through the Fußgangerzone and up to Kaufland. We bought peterseli and tomato paste, and that was it. Tomorrow we go back and buy milk and meat and some other things. That makes me mad, because we aren't using our time wisely. I need to work on that. Anyhow. After that we had lunch and then did a few zurückkehrens and a bit of TzT. Not to much luck there. We came home about 1700 so Elder C could prepare for Institute. Then at 1800 was the big Spiel des Lebens! I think it went pretty well. Herr K was there to grill the Würstchen, and Frau K came also. Frau P and her brother didn't come though. It was a lot of fun. I played a lot with the K kids, the K kids, and D's. Mostly with M, D, and M. Wow kids! I was mostly doing this swingy thing with D. It helped though. Now the K kids really like us and Frau K also. I hope that will help us help them. Well, after all the fun we helped Brd. J clean up.

All in all, it was a good day. Lots of fun and excitement!
