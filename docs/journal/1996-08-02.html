Can't keep my head straight

Friday, Aug 02, 1996
Location: Halberstadt

Today went a lot better than yesterday. It was still hot, but I brought water with me. So instead of heat sucking all the water out of me, it sucked all the energy out of me. I was dead and my feet were killing for most of the day.

To make it all even better, I was really trying to memorize the discussions, but I couldn't keep my might straight and on one thing for more than a few seconds. It really made me mad because I'm really trying to memorize them, but I can't keep my head straight. I'm really excited to get a bike. It probably won't make it any easier, but it'll be nevertheless exciting.
