I try to translate into English

Sunday, Aug 25, 1996
Location: Halberstadt

This was a good Sunday. It started off awesome because Herr K came to church. He could only stay for sacrament because his wife was feeling ill, but he still came! We didn't get to make out a new appointment, because he is too busy for the next two weeks.

I tried to translate today for Ashley in Sunday school. It didn't work to well. I don't understand enough myself to be able to translate. Elder C or J will translate next week.

We got food from Familie S and ate here alone. I made mashed potatoes, and we had them with the meat and vegetables. The meat was good and so were the potatoes.

After lunch we did some door to door on Großringstraße. On the way there I felt real light headed. Luckily I didn't faint or anything, and that it only lasted until we visited H. We visited him before we door to door. We also made an appointment with him for Friday.

The clouds were neat while doing door to door. big, dark clouds, with lots of wind, too. It only rained a little bit though.

After TzT we sang with Bruder J. B sang a few songs with us too. That's all of the highlight, even though they are boring, for today.
