Germany Hamburg, April 10, 1996 - April 4, 1998
HOME JOURNAL

Just one of those days...

Monday, Sep 23, 1996
Location: Halberstadt

Today was a normal weird day. It started out with us being an half-hour late to Herr R's. He ended up not being there anyhow, so it didn't matter. Then, right after that, as we were walking through the Fussgänger Zone, a lady stopped us and tried to give us J.W. stuff. We said we would take it if she would take a Book of Mormon. She of course wouldn't, so we didn't. Then at the other end of the Fussgänger Zone some guy yelled a scripture at us and starting talking to us. I didn't understand him at all. When he was done talking he thanked us for the conversation and took off. After that we went to Rossmans and left our film to be developed. I also bought an Überraschungs Eir and got a weeny prize. Then we went to Postamt and bought some Briefmarken. We saw A there.

After all that fun, we helped Frau F and then went to Frau K. We ended up being there for 4 hours. 2 hours with Frau K and 2 hours with P. Then we came home and ate tomato soup with biscuits. They tasted good and filled me up. The only problem with that was that we had Abendbrot by B's. We ended up just listening to Sch. B because Brd. B was really tired and went to bed right after we got there.

All in all, today was...! Just one of those days!
