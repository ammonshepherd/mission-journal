Germany Hamburg, April 10, 1996 - April 4, 1998
HOME JOURNAL

I ate corn on the cob

Saturday, Oct 12, 1996
Location: Halberstadt

Today went alright. We read in the Book of Mormon with Brd. J this morning. After that we came home and had pizza for Mittags. After Mittags we started moving some stuff from the other apartment up. We moved a Schrank up, and also a lot of papers. Mostly die Sterns and some pamphlets and stuff. That was done until about 17:40, and then we went to B's for Abendbrot. We had taco salad and corn on the cob. I actually ate a corn on the cob. It even tasted o.k. until about half way through. It tasted gross but I finished it. While there a funny movie came on and we ended up staying until it was over (10:00pm). J, B, her brother, M and her boyfriend were all there. We got home at 10:10 or so, so that's not too bad.

The only thing is, is that I should have started fasting after Mittags, but because we went to B's I couldn't. I plan on starting my fast, then, tomorrow after Mittags, but if K gives us food when we go on our little venture, then I'll have to push it back another day.

I think everything is back to good with Elder N and I. I think he realized what I really meant and wanted to say. He is really a good guy. Na ja!

I hope I get transferred in Nov. 5 months in one place is a long time.
