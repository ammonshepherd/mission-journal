Mission Journal

Germany Hamburg, April 10, 1996 - April 4, 1998
HOME JOURNAL

Losing keys again and again

Saturday, Jul 13, 1996
Location: Halberstadt

This day was a pretty busy day. First off, I mailed off my packet home, the one with the shirt and tape and stuff. It cost 12DM by ship or 24DM by plane; 3 weeks by ship and only 1 1/2 or so by plane. I picked the ship. Anyhow, after that we met with Bro. J. I got a couple of pictures, too.

Then we came home and got lunch fixed for us and P and B. Elder C didn't have his key for the basement so we all ate up here in our apartment. That's really against the rules, but what could we do?

Then B dropped us off in Quedlinburg and we did some visiting there. We visited K S, a member, and a few other contacts we had made before doing street contacting. One seemed kind of interested, we can come back next month, the other one had no time yet, but we can come back next weekend.

Then we did some Tür zu Tür. P just sat in the background most of the time. And for the two streets we did, we had a neighbor kid follow us around.

After all that fun we got a ride home with B and we actually came home and didn't hang out forever. That made me happy.

Elder C and I locked ourselves out of our apartment but we were able to get back in quickly.

Today went by really well. My feet were sore and I was tired, so I guess we worked hard enough.
