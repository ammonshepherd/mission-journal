Germany Hamburg, April 10, 1996 - April 4, 1998
HOME JOURNAL

Cleaning the bathroom and kitchen

Saturday, Aug 10, 1996
Location: Halberstadt

Today went pretty well. It was a nice laid back day. I didn't like that though. We spent the first half of the morning cleaning up the other Elder's Wohnung real well. I did the bathroom and kitchen. I got a few things, too. I got a scarf, two pairs of gloves, and a rain jacket. I know I'm blessed because I didn't bring any thing like that with me. I need to ask the family to send me my own gloves.

Well, after all that clean fun we had Mittags and then worked on finding zurückkehrens for 2 hours. After that we went to a few of them.

We were planning on buying a Dönner to celebrate Elder C's birthday today, but we were both full still from lunch so we're waiting until Tuesday, when we tausch mit Elder J and V.

Also, cool news for today, I passed off the first discussion! Now I'm going to try the 2nd in 11 days, before Zone Conference.
